Working with Llama 3.1, the Infosys team says open source is the future Our approach Research Product experiences Llama Blog Try Meta AI Large Language Model Working with Llama 3.1, the Infosys team says open source is the future August 28, 2024 • 2 minute read For the team at Infosys , a global leader in next-generation digital services and consulting, open source is the future. Using Llama 3.1 , they’ve built a variety of agent-based AI models and retrieval augmented generation (RAG) systems for processing documents, videos, audio, and more. The company provides the services as part of Infosys Topaz , an AI-first offering used to accelerate business value for global enterprises. The Infosys team initially learned about Llama on Hugging Face and, since then, has steadily added use cases for internal operations, starting with documents and progressing to analyzing videos, images, and structured data. On the Infosys Topaz platform, Llama is used with a variety of document types, including legal contracts, invoices, purchase orders, research documents, and training videos. An early adopter of Llama 3.1, Infosys is leveraging its capabilities to unlock new efficiencies as part of the company’s commitment to staying at the forefront of open source. “Open source is fun and helps us sharpen our skills,” says Sandro Bovelli, gen AI architect at Infosys. “The open source ecosystem allows us to get our hands dirty with some of the best technologies without spending a fortune. We are extremely happy with it.” Infosys also leverages Llama 3 as part of its in-house legal assistant that uses RAG. When someone asks a question, they’re provided with information and cited sources, which boosts the trust of the people who use it. Keeping security a priority, the legal assistant is deployed on a GPU cluster within Infosys firewalls and helps provide cutting-edge gen AI capabilities to Infosys users securely and confidentially. "AI is for everyone" Many emerging technologies have their roots in open source, Bovelli notes. As enterprises expand their generative AI landscape, open source plays a vital role in collaboration, transparency, and innovation. The team at Infosys also believes iit can level the playing field. “Typically, smaller companies wouldn’t have the resources to step back and respond to unexpected technological developments, putting them at a disadvantage compared to their larger competitors,” says Tushar Kesarkar, industry principal at Infosys. “Sometimes it may seem like a full-time job just to keep abreast of the latest developments in the space.” Kesarkar says the best approach is to dive in, learn, and be open to ways AI can help. "You don’t have to stand on the sidelines of gen AI and wonder in amazement. AI is for everyone.
