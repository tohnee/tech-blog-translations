# 有哪些学习深度学习的好书或好论文？

> 原文：[What are some good books/papers for learning deep learning?](https://sebastianraschka.com/faq/docs/deep-learning-resources.html) · Sebastian Raschka's FAQ

一篇很好的概述与入门材料是最近发表在 Nature 上的深度学习综述（http://www.nature.com/nature/journal/v521/n7553/full/nature14539.html）；其中引用了大量值得跟进的有用文献

- LeCun, Yann, Yoshua Bengio, and Geoffrey Hinton. "Deep learning." Nature521.7553 (2015): 436-444.

作为很好的教科书资源，我想推荐 Yoshua Bengio 即将出版的《Deep Learning》一书。该书可免费访问：http://goodfeli.github.io/dlbook/

- Bengio, Yoshua, Ian Goodfellow, and Aaron Courville. "Deep learning." An MIT Press book in preparation. Draft chapters available at http://www. iro. umontreal. ca/∼ bengioy/dlbook (2014).
