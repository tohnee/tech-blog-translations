# MAI Realtime 1: Leading voice AI at $0.01 per audio minute

MAI Realtime 1, our first speech-to-speech model, is built for naturally flowing conversations in real-world enterprise applications starting at **$0.01 per audio minute**.[1](#fn-1)

Our model outperforms GPT Realtime 2.1 on quality, speed, and price across public benchmarks and Microsoft product evaluations. It sits on the performance-price Pareto frontier for both Big Bench Audio speech reasoning and Tau-Voice agentic task completion benchmarks.[2](#fn-2)

## Natural conversations that get things done

Building a voice agent people actually want to talk to takes more than just a good voice. It takes the right mix of conversation, intelligence, and action working simultaneously. MAI Realtime 1 brings that together through the following capabilities:

* **Natural conversation:** MAI Realtime 1 manages interruptions, handles quick acknowledgments, and knows when to wait for someone to finish speaking

* **Adaptive intelligence**: A fast, responsive system keeps the conversation flowing in real time. When a task requires deeper thinking, it taps into a more powerful reasoning engine, delivering thoughtful answers without awkward pauses

* **Robust understanding:** It recognizes requests even with background noise or other people talking

* **Real-world action:** It takes on complex requests and connects to enterprise systems and tools, so voice agents can do more than simply answer questions

* **Adaptable personas:** It adjusts tone, style, and behavior through prompting to suit different customers, domains, and use cases

MAI Realtime 1 is also compatible with the OpenAI Realtime API, making it straightforward for developers to test the model and migrate existing voice applications.

## Ready for real-world applications

Companies including Agoda and Reservations.ai are already putting MAI Realtime 1 to the test.

We’re also bringing MAI Realtime 1 to Dynamics 365 Contact Center for customer service applications, and to Dragon Copilot, to support AI-simulated patient conversations that help nurses practice clinical interactions.
