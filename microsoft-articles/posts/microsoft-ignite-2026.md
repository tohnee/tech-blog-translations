# Seven new world-class models at scalable price-performance

At MAI, our mission is to build you the very best models in the world and keep every developer and every enterprise at the frontier of capability.

But as token spend skyrockets, we know that you also want models at a price point that lets you run agents 24/7 doing real engineering work.

That’s why today we’re releasing a family of seven new world class models at a truly exceptional price-performance.

**Here’s the lineup:**

* **[MAI Cyber 2 Flash](/news/introducing-mai-cyber-2-flash/)** beats GPT-6 Astra’s quality at less than 2% of the cost per task on the CyberGym-E2E benchmark.
* **[MAI Code 2](/news/introducing-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash/)** reaches 98% of the coding quality of GPT-6.1 Sol at about 40% of the price. And against Opus 5.5, it reaches 96% performance for just 5% of the price.
* **[MAI Code 2 Flash](/news/introducing-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash/)** shows comparable performance GPT-6 Luna at 65% of the price, and Haiku 5.5 at 20% of the price.
* **[MAI Image 2.7](/news/introducing-mai-image-2-7/)** is #1 on Artificial Analysis image editing, outperforming GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (max) at just one-fifth of the price.
* **[MAI Realtime 1](/news/introducing-mai-realtime-1/)** outperforms GPT-Realtime 2.1 and starts at just $0.01 per audio minute.
* **Dragon Copilot Transcribe** is built on MAI Transcribe 2, the world’s fastest and most accurate multilingual transcription model, and already powers 10 million patient encounters a month with multilingual error rates halved.
* **[Decision 1.1](http://aka.ms/decision-model-ga)** is the latest decision model from Microsoft, 50x faster and 79x cheaper than GPT-6.1 Sol.
* [**Maia 200**](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/12/microsoft-to-expand-azure-maia-system-to-multi-gigawatt-deployments/), Microsoft’s second-generation AI accelerator, is now in production running our largest models, achieving 1.7x more tokens per watt.

Security, alignment and containment are the most important priorities for agent development in 2027 and beyond. That’s our top priority. We need to run monitoring and control agents defending and patching live production systems 24/7. That’s why **[MAI Cyber 2 Flash](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-cyber-2-flash/)** was designed to deliver frontier intelligence at a fraction of the cost.

Built with Microsoft’s decades of proprietary data and world-class security expertise, our models help defenders stay safe in this new era where attackers have cheap and abundant offensive tools.

MAI Cyber 2 Flash builds on earlier MAI Cyber models that defenders already rely on. MAI Cyber accounts for almost 40% of scans in MDASH, and 90% of scans that started with MAI continued to use it. Our latest model is now priced 86% lower on output tokens than MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash, giving existing MDASH customers a stronger model at a lower price.

## Incredible coding performance at a fraction of the cost

**[MAI Code 2](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash/)** is built for the best price-performance for everyday enterprise coding tasks. Averaged over four widely used benchmarks that target the everyday enterprise coding tasks we see in GitHub Copilot (DeepSWE 1.1, Terminal Bench 2.1, SWE-Atlas and FrontierCode v1.1), MAI Code 2 gives you 98% of the performance of GPT-6.1 Sol at 40% of the price, and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5’s performance at just 5% of the price.

We’ve built these models to prioritize the coding work our customers do every day. They’re explicitly not yet tuned for harder problems, like fundamental science research and very long horizon agentic work.

MAI Code 2 also been tuned to generate great dashboards and visualizations. In a blind, side-by-side evaluation of frontend work, developers preferred MAI Code 2 over GPT-6 Sol 64% of the time. Try MAI Code 2 in [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) today. [Read more here](/news/introducing-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash/).

[**MAI Code 2 Flash**](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash/) is designed for high-volume, everyday tasks like code explanations, targeted edits, routine fixes, and rapid iterations, while bringing quality at a much lower cost. It performs comparably to GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 at 65% and 20% of the price, respectively, across three coding benchmarks measuring day-to-day, high-volume routine coding tasks (DeepSWE 1.1, Terminal Bench 2.1 and SWE-Atlas). It’s available in [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) and in a limited-access preview in [Microsoft Foundry](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/ai-foundry).
