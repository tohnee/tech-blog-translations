# MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash: Sharper findings and fewer tokens inside MDASH

October 27, 2026 

Models


![A shield-shaped icon with a watercolor texture in pastel yellow, pink, and blue against a soft, pink, flowing background.](https://microsoft.ai/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/health_shield_pink.webp)

MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash takes vulnerability discovery a step forward, delivering **comparable performance** to MAI Cyber 1 Flash while **costing 18% less per scan** on an internal MDASH vulnerability-scanning evaluation suite.

With [MAI Cyber 1 Flash](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-cyber-1-flash-inside-mdash/), we showed what’s possible when a specialized security model, Microsoft’s security data, and the MDASH harness work together.

MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash builds on that foundation. It’s a more focused model that takes on more of the work itself, so the full MDASH system can surface useful findings at lower cost.


## **More efficient scans**

MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash is built to do more with less. On the same MDASH evaluation suite, each scan costs 18% less to complete on an internal product evaluation set.

The goal is bigger than making an individual model cheaper to run, making the entire vulnerability discovery system more efficient while preserving the performance defenders rely on.

## **Built on customer momentum**

Customers who try MAI Cyber 1 Flash in MDASH tend to stick with it. After running their first scan with the MAI Cyber 1 Flash profile, nearly **9 in 10 subsequent scans use the MAI profile rather than the OpenAI-only model profile**.

To date, MAI Cyber now **accounts for almost** **50% of all MDASH scans** with over thousands of scans from hundreds of customers. MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash brings those customers a more focused, efficient model within the workflow they *already* use.


## **Available to MDASH customers**

MAI Cyber 1.1 Flash is available through MDASH to verified customers, with safeguards in place to support responsible use, including clear usage policies and ongoing monitoring.
