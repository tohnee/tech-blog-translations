# MAI Image 2.7: Frontier quality at a fraction of the cost

Today we’re introducing **MAI Image 2.7** and **MAI Image 2.7 Flash**, delivering frontier image generation and editing at a fraction of the cost of the leading competitors. We’re also launching **[Canvas](https://labs.microsoft.ai/?model=canvas)**, a new interactive workspace powered by our Flash model to create, edit, and refine images.

MAI Image 2.7 takes the #1 spot on Artificial Analysis image editing, outperforming GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (max) at just one-fifth of the cost. On Arena, MAI is a top-two lab in both image generation and editing, outperforming GPT Image 2.6 Flare [1](#fn-1). Flash delivers the speed and efficiency needed for production workloads, with both models on the quality-cost Pareto frontier for image generation and editing.

Available in public preview on Microsoft Foundry, MAI Image 2.7 costs ~$35 per 1,000 images while Flash costs ~$10 per 1,000 images.[2](#fn-2)

## Fine-grained edit control

Change an object’s color, material, or position without disrupting the rest of an image. MAI Image 2.7 preserves visual consistency across successive edits, allowing creators to refine results and explore variations without starting over.

Both models support transparent backgrounds for ready-to-use product images, logos, icons, and illustrations without manual cutouts or cleanup.

## **Create and refine with Canvas**

Canvas is a new interactive experience in MAI Labs, powered by MAI Image 2.7 Flash. Start with a prompt or sketch, turn rough ideas into polished diagrams, then select any part of an image to replace, move, or restyle, all in one space.

## **Built for creators and developers**

MAI Image is now the default model family powering [Bing Image Creator](https://www.bing.com/images/create/ai-image-generator), with usage growing at a compounded weekly growth rate of ~15% since June.[3](#fn-3)

Creative platforms and businesses including Adobe, Figma Weave, Minute (Sports Illustrated), Wirestock, and Manus are also working with MAI Image.

## **Get started**

Try MAI Image 2.7 and MAI Image 2.7 Flash in [MAI Labs](https://labs.microsoft.ai/?model=mai-image-2-7-flash), including [Canvas](https://labs.microsoft.ai/?model=canvas).

Both models are available in public preview through [Microsoft Foundry](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/ai-foundry) and partners including [fal](https://fal.ai/explore/search), [Genspark](https://www.genspark.ai/), [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/models), and [Vercel](https://vercel.com/).
