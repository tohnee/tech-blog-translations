### INFUSE: A Unified Surface Benchmark for Indirect Prompt Injection in Agentic Systems

> Authors: Thuy Dung Nguyen, Jingyi Guo, Rana Seda Dubauskas, Sreepriya Damuluru, Taylor T. Johnson, Kevin Leach<br> Affiliations: Vanderbilt University

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20776v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20776v1)

### 论文摘要

LLM-based agents increasingly execute long-horizon, end-to-end workflows involving shell commands, code modification, dependency installation, and interaction with remote services. This growing autonomy expands their exposure to indirect prompt injection (IPI), where adversarial instructions embedded in external content are interpreted as legitimate task directives. Existing evaluations, however, often focus on isolated malicious strings, single-step tool calls, or narrowly scoped agent configurations. We introduce INFUSE, an end-to-end benchmark for evaluating IPI throughout the lifecycle of long-horizon agent workflows. INFUSE comprises 40 realistic tasks and a structured taxonomy of 56 attack scenarios spanning five threat categories. Attacks are injected through realistic interaction surfaces encountered during task execution, extending evaluation beyond attacks placed only in initial prompts or isolated tool interactions. We evaluate 19 LLMs from the GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Gemma, and Qwen families, producing 41,824 adversarial trajectories (26,220 without defenses) and 720 benign reference runs. Across the benchmark, mean attack success reaches 19.6% under LLM-based evaluation and 15.4% under heuristic verification, with per-model ASR-L ranging from 3.2% to 41.9%. Existing guard models provide limited protection, while prompt-level instructional hardening reduces mean attack success from 58.2% to 14.3%, revealing substantial but model-dependent security-utility trade-offs. Our code is available at [https://github.com/judydnguyen/infuse](https://github.com/judydnguyen/infuse).

**论文简评**: 该论文关注于大模型代理系统在实际端到端工作流程中面临的间接提示注入（IPI）威胁。动机在于现有安全评测只覆盖有限场景，无法真实反映代理系统在多交互、多表面环境下的脆弱性。作者提出了INFUSE基准，涵盖40个真实任务和56种攻击场景，并通过多表面注入引擎在文件名、内容、配置、终端输出等位置植入恶意指令。实验覆盖19个主流LLM模型，采集41824条攻击轨迹，发现平均攻击成功率为19.6%（LLM评判）和15.4%（启发式验证），防御模型效果有限，提示级硬化可将攻击成功率显著降至14.3%，但伴随明显的效用损失。整体工作揭示了大模型代理在长时序复杂环境下的安全–效用权衡，并为后续安全研究提供了统一、可扩展的评测框架。

### Learning When to Withdraw: Curriculum Coverage and Rule-Specific Failures in Fine-Tuned Language Agents

> Authors: Mohamed Abouzahra<br> Affiliations: California State University, Monterey Bay

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21045v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21045v1)

### 论文摘要

Tool-using language agents must revise prepared actions when evidence changes or authority is revoked. Selective withdrawal means suspending affected actions, preserving supported work, and resuming only after sufficient repair. We study supervised fine-tuning in NAQD-Env, a synthetic environment with explicit dependencies and a deterministic reference policy. Four LoRA checkpoints of Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct compare an original curriculum with an enriched one, the latter under three system-prompt framings with identical views and targets. Three instruction-tuned baselines and all four checkpoints are evaluated under three inference prompts on fresh validation cases, held-out dependency families, and held-out structural combinations. Under the naqd prompt, SFT checkpoints reach decision agreement of 0.77-0.92 and withdrawal recall of 0.46-0.86, versus 0.45-0.63 and at most 0.06 for the baselines. Rule-level diagnostics qualify these gains. The original-curriculum checkpoint wrongly withdraws 26 of 34 actions with surviving alternative support; enriched checkpoints withdraw 0-1. Conversely, all SFT checkpoints narrow prohibited combinations less often than the 7B and 8B baselines, a case absent from both curricula. Delegation results show more withdrawal but poorer preservation of independently authorized actions, and all four checkpoints omit valid event reports. Framing contrasts vary across metrics and splits and do not establish a reproducible advantage or equivalence. One training run per arm, jointly changed curriculum properties, prior evaluation exposure, and baseline-SFT quantization differences limit causal attribution. The contribution is a method for detecting rule-specific regressions that aggregate accuracy can conceal, not evidence that fine-tuning alone ensures agent safety.

**论文简评**: 本文关注于具备工具调用能力的语言智能体在环境依赖发生变更或权限被撤销时，如何实现“选择性回退”——即只撤回受影响的操作、保留仍有支持的行动，并在修复依赖后恢复工作。作者在NAQD-Env合成环境中，针对Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct模型，通过原始与增强两种课程（curriculum）和多种系统提示（framing）下的LoRA微调，系统比较了微调前后智能体在依赖撤销、授权继承、组合泛化等多种细粒度规则下的行为表现。实验结果显示，经过增强课程微调的智能体在聚合准确率和依赖回退召回率上显著优于基线模型，并能有效避免“无关操作被错误撤销”等局部失误，但在部分任务（如独立授权保留、事件报告）上仍有缺陷。论文提出了细粒度规则级回归检测方法，强调单纯的聚合指标可能掩盖微调带来的潜在风险，为后续智能体安全性评价和微调方案设计提供了更具诊断性的工具。

### Independent Data-Integrity Verification for LLM-Driven Functional Coverage Closure in Hardware Design

> Authors: Venkata Krishna Kumar Vedantam<br> Affiliations: Portland State University

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21570v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21570v1)

### 论文摘要

LLM agents can write directed tests that close functional coverage, but coverage only shows that a state was reached, not that the data on the way there was correct. This is a long-known point in verification and we do not claim it as new. We describe a small coverage-closure agent that applies it to an LLM loop: coverage is read from the report that the simulation itself exports (cocotb-coverage YAML) and never taken from the model’s account, and data integrity is checked by scoreboards that are separate from the model. On three architecturally distinct RTL designs (an asynchronous FIFO, a UART transmitter and an AHB2APB bridge) the agent raised coverage from 91.7%, 88.9% and 67.4% to 100% within its 8-iteration budget in every logged run, using one design-agnostic agent. A reactive scoreboard monitor attached to the FIFO’s base testbench also found a clock-domain-crossing bug in the author’s own stimulus code. The monitor is not yet attached to the LLM-generated tests, so their data integrity is not independently checked; one manual check on a single FIFO test is reported as preliminary. We list the weaknesses of this evidence: three designs, no baseline, no ablation, and logged runs that come from several agent checkpoints.

**论文简评**: 本文关注于如何在大语言模型（LLM）驱动的硬件功能覆盖闭环流程中实现独立的数据完整性验证。动机在于，虽然LLM能够生成用于提升功能覆盖率的定向测试，但仅有覆盖率并不能保证数据路径的正确性。作者提出了一种将覆盖率获取与数据完整性检查分离的小型agent框架：覆盖率直接从仿真报告读取，数据完整性通过独立scoreboard检测。方法在三个不同结构的RTL设计上（异步FIFO、UART发射器和AHB2APB桥）实验，均能在8轮内将覆盖率从91.7%、88.9%、67.4%提升至100%，并能通过scoreboard检测发现实际测试激励中的时序错误。尽管工作验证了agent在提升覆盖率和发现bug方面的有效性，但目前LLM生成的测试尚未在循环中自动进行数据完整性独立校验，实验范围也局限于三个小型设计，未来计划扩展自动监测和基线对比实验。

### DocPilot: Interactive Agent Orchestration and Training for Long Visual Document Understanding

> Authors: Zhuowen Liang, Xiaotian Lin, Yuyu Luo, Haixun Wang, Nan Tang<br> Affiliations: The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou); Shanghai Jiao Tong University; University of California, San Diego; The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21327v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21327v1)

### 论文摘要

Multimodal long-document question answering requires models to seek sparse evidence across pages and modalities, understand heterogeneous document content, and reason over the collected evidence. Even advanced vision-language models (VLMs) can overlook sparse evidence and struggle with cross-modal reasoning in a single pass. Existing document agents further introduce multi-step interaction, but some still rely on predefined workflows or coarse-grained actions, limiting the flexibility and granularity of document reasoning. We introduce DocPilot, an agentic document framework that supports expressive orchestration and makes complex document solving learnable by small language models (SLMs). DocPilot orchestrates evidence seeking through page-level navigation and fine-grained evidence localization, and integrates local visual inspection, code-based numerical reasoning, and cross-modal reasoning for multimodal understanding. To transfer these capabilities to small models, we propose curriculum-based agentic training that progresses from single-ability learning to adaptive orchestration through supervision from data-grounded reasoning traces and executable interaction trajectories. Across MMLongBench-Doc, DocBench, FinRAGBench-V, and MADQA, DocPilot achieves strong gains with frontier VLM backbones, while DocPilot-4B delivers competitive performance against substantially larger models, demonstrating strong effectiveness and generalization across diverse long-document reasoning settings. The code is available at [https://github.com/HKUSTDial/DocPilot](https://github.com/HKUSTDial/DocPilot).

![](https://pic4.zhimg.com/v2-9bdd5d983f97687c5abc5406425858f3_r.jpg)

**论文简评**: 本论文针对多模态长文档问答任务中证据分散、跨模态推理难等问题，提出了具备丰富交互与编排能力的智能文档代理框架DocPilot。其创新之处在于通过细粒度的行动（如页面导航、证据抽取、局部视觉聚焦、代码推理等）实现自适应证据搜寻和多模态理解，并引入基于课程学习的训练范式，将复杂文档推理能力迁移到小型语言模型。实验在MMLongBench-Doc、DocBench、FinRAGBench-V和MADQA等多项长文档问答基准上，DocPilot在多种主流大模型骨干上均取得显著性能提升，且仅4B参数的DocPilot-4B即达到了与更大模型接近或超越的效果，展现出优异的高效性和泛化能力。

### Athanor: An Agent-Native System for Autonomous Post-Training Research

> Authors: Yufa Zhou, Yichen Wang, Jiakun Fan, Ziqi Shu, Zhichao Liu, Anru R. Zhang, Neiwen Ling, Zijun Wei<br> Affiliations: ByteDance; Duke University; The University of Chicago; Virginia Tech

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21055v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21055v1)

### 论文摘要

Recursive self-improvement, in which AI systems build stronger AI systems, is one promising route to superintelligence. We study a weaker form that can be measured today: frontier agents post-training a small raw base model with public data, as a test of their research potential. We build Athanor, an open agent-native system for autonomous post-training research (AutoPostTrain) on real GPUs. We give the agents full research autonomy, low-friction engineering tools, and efficient starter code. The agents carry out the research themselves: they read datasets and evaluation results, build their training data, write and modify training algorithms, tune hyperparameters, and analyze the results to decide what to try next. They work in a sandbox and cannot use external teacher models. Athanor’s services supply frozen evaluation, managed jobs, decontaminated data, a persistent research record, and a held-out suite the agents are unaware of. In a 104-hour campaign, a team of four Claude Opus 5.5 agents, each on an eight-GPU node and given no research advice, improves Qwen3-4B-Base from 15.9 to 67.3 on a six-task development suite and from 9.2 to 48.2 on a held-out suite, against 66.5 and 47.9 for the official Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507. To our knowledge, this is the first demonstration that a team of frontier agents can autonomously post-train a raw base model into a cross-domain generalist model that matches or surpasses the official instruct version on average. The campaign’s full record shows that the agents reach this level by recombining known methods and each other’s models: SFT on public reasoning data, RL with a rollout change one agent introduced, weight merges across agents, and rounds of self-distillation. With execution handled, today’s agents already have the engineering and research ability to conquer this task. We hope Athanor provides a foundation for scaling autonomous AI research.

![](https://pic2.zhimg.com/v2-1849ac82eaa22f65047917559b54998d_r.jpg)

**论文简评**: 本论文关注AI代理自主进行大模型后训练研究的能力，提出了Athanor系统以支持前沿代理在真实GPU环境下自主完成后训练（AutoPostTrain），包括数据处理、训练算法编写、超参数调优和结果分析，且全程不依赖外部教师模型。动机在于检验代理的科学研究潜力和递归自改进能力，通过剥离执行与决策、外部化研究状态和提升研究流程生产力，Athanor为代理提供低摩擦工程工具和完整研究记录。在实验中，四个Claude Opus 5.5代理团队用104小时将Qwen3-4B-Base的多任务得分从15.9提升至67.3（开发集），并在未见过的持出集上达到48.2，超越或匹配官方Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507模型。分析显示代理能够重组已知方法、团队协作、早期收敛和数据质量把控，验证了当前代理具备自主工程和研究能力。Athanor为未来自主AI研究的规模化提供了基础和范例。

### Believed but Not Said: Measuring Hidden Belief Collapse in Multi-Agent LLM Discussions

> Authors: Liangjie Huang, Henry Peng Zou, Wei-Chieh Huang, Wooseong Yang, Yuwei Han, Philip S. Yu<br> Affiliations: University of Illinois Chicago

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22138v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22138v1)

### 论文摘要

LLM systems increasingly rely on groups of agents that discuss, critique, and check one another, assuming that peers will catch individual errors. We test this assumption against a minimal adversary. In a ten-agent factual discussion, one or three adversarial agents repeatedly assert a plausible false answer. After each round we probe each benign agent’s private answer through a separate call whose response is hidden from its peers, and we separately label its public stance toward the claim. Across six models and three factual-QA datasets, groups remain stable without an attacker: only 0.6% of benign agents finish with the false answer. A single adversarial agent increases this rate to 44.8% and three to 60.5%. Studies of conformity typically report public agreement without private change; we find the reverse. With three adversarial agents present, benign agents hold the false answer in 51.6% of exposed turns but publicly endorse it in only 19.7%. Much of the influence therefore remains invisible in the discussion, and the false answers persist when agents are asked for their own views. Agents that retain the true answer often fail to defend it; once adopted, the false answer spreads among benign agents, and baseline knowledge does not predict robustness.

![](https://pica.zhimg.com/v2-1fe1c4f696df1395db51b578f7abcd6a_r.jpg)

**论文简评**: 本论文关注多代理大语言模型（LLM）系统在群体讨论中对错误信息的鲁棒性，尤其研究少数敌对代理在群体中持续传播虚假答案时，对其他代理“私有信念”而非“公开表态”的影响。作者提出了双通道测量协议，分别记录每轮讨论中代理的公开发言和私下答案，并在六种模型、三类事实问答数据集上开展实验。结果显示：无攻击者时，代理群体几乎不会采纳错误答案；一到三个攻击者则导致高达44.8%到60.5%的代理在私下认同错误答案，但公开表态远低于这一比例，隐藏信念崩塌严重且难以通过群聊内容察觉。进一步分析发现，知识丰富的模型并不一定更鲁棒，信念崩塌会迅速蔓延且难以逆转，强调了在多代理系统设计和评估中，需关注代理的私有信念和早期干预，而非仅凭公开讨论结果。

### Physical AI: A Survey of Models, Learning, Systems, and Open Challenges

> Authors: Bin Gao, Anuj Gautam, Aritra Bagchi, Yunkai Liang, Wentao Li, Ronghui Li, Zhi Zhou, Yancan Mao, Xiao Yan, Xin He, Qingyun Zou, Wenshuo Yue, Dan Chen, Zhanglu Yan, Fei Xu, Huize Li, Weijie Wang, Tao Ouyang, Tulika Mitra<br> Affiliations: National University of Singapore; Indian Institute of Technology Bombay; Sun Yat-sen University; Huazhong University of Science and Technology; Wuhan University; East China Normal University; University of Central Florida; Fondazione Bruno Kessler; Central South University

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21171v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21171v1)

### 论文摘要

Physical AI aims to enable intelligent systems to perceive, reason, act, and learn through interaction with the physical world. Unlike purely digital intelligence, physical agents must operate under partial observability, uncertain dynamics, embodiment constraints, and real-time requirements, while their actions continuously reshape the environment and subsequent observations. Recent advances in foundation models and robot learning have expanded the capabilities of physical agents, yet achieving reliable autonomy requires more than improvements in individual models. This survey presents a comprehensive, system-level review of Physical AI, organized around the closed-loop process of physical interaction. We examine the foundations and recent developments in task representations and memory, predictive and action-generating models, data and learning methods, embodiment and control, computing systems, evaluation, and safety. Beyond reviewing individual advances, we investigate how these components interact and collectively shape the capabilities and limitations of Physical AI systems. Building on this integrated perspective, we identify fundamental challenges in generalization, resource-efficient learning and execution, reliable state tracking, safety, recovery, and continual adaptation. We argue that advancing Physical AI requires moving beyond isolated model performance toward integrated systems capable of robust, efficient, safe, and autonomous physical interaction under changing real-world conditions.

**论文简评**: 本论文系统性回顾了“物理智能”（Physical AI）领域，聚焦于机器人等物理体智能体在真实世界中感知、推理、行动和学习所面临的挑战。作者指出，物理智能与纯数字智能不同，需应对信息不完备、动态不确定、物理约束和实时性等难题。论文围绕物理交互的闭环过程，梳理了任务表示、预测与行动模型、数据与学习方法、具身控制、计算系统、评估与安全等关键环节，并讨论了它们之间的系统级依赖与协同。实验和文献分析显示，单一模型的进步难以带来可靠自主性，只有各子系统的集成优化才能实现稳健、高效、安全和自适应的物理智能。作者最后总结了泛化、资源高效、可靠状态估计、安全与持续适应等亟待突破的开放挑战，为推动该领域的发展提供了系统视角和研究路线图。

### Mem4Act: Learning Effective Latent Memory for Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation

> Authors: Yun Zhang, Zhiyu Huang, Tianhui Cai, Chen Tang, Wei Wang, Jiaqi Ma<br> Affiliations: University of California, Los Angeles; North Carolina State University

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22323v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22323v1)

### 论文摘要

Long-horizon robotic manipulation fundamentally requires memory: agents must retain task-relevant information from past observations and interactions and use it to guide future actions. Existing approaches typically preserve raw historical observations, compressed visual tokens, or language summaries, but these representations either expose the action policy to increasingly long histories or risk discarding task-relevant temporal, semantic, and perceptual information. We introduce Mem4Act, a framework for learning effective latent memory as a fixed-size representation explicitly optimized for long-horizon robotic control. Mem4Act addresses three coupled challenges: how to efficiently cover long histories, what information memory should retain, and how to ensure the policy actually relies on memory. To this end, it organizes history via multi-granularity temporal aggregation, preserves complementary semantic and perceptual information through question-answering supervision and key-frame DINO feature reconstruction, and employs memory-first two-stage training to encourage greater use of memory by the action policy. The resulting memory captures both high-level task state and fine-grained perceptual evidence while providing a bounded interface to the action policy. Across RoboMME, RMBench, and four real-world manipulation tasks, Mem4Act achieves state-of-the-art performance on memory-dependent control, with success rates of 49.88%, 61.0%, and 81.5%, respectively. Extensive ablations further demonstrate the importance of temporal aggregation, complementary memory supervision, and memory-first training.

![](https://pic2.zhimg.com/v2-088f030259aaee5bca1a6271ef685f33_r.jpg)

**论文简评**: 本文针对机器人长时序操控任务中有效利用历史信息的需求，提出了Mem4Act框架，通过结构化的多粒度时序聚合方法，将历史观测压缩为固定容量的潜在记忆表示。为提升记忆内容的有效性，Mem4Act结合了语义问答和关键帧视觉特征重建的辅助监督，并采用两阶段“记忆优先”训练策略，先训练历史记忆，再联合动作策略优化。实验在RoboMME、RMBench和真实双臂机器人任务上验证了方法的优越性，均显著优于同类SOTA模型，展示了结构化潜在记忆在长时序、部分可观测机器人任务中的重要作用。

### DialFork: Going Beyond Factuality to Evaluate Procedural Hallucination in Conversational Agents

> Authors: Sergio Burdisso, Esaú Villatoro-Tello, Dairazalia Sánchez-Cortés, Yanis Labrak, Jordi Luque, Milos Cernak, Petr Motlicek<br> Affiliations: Idiap Research Institute; Logitech; Telefónica Innovación Digital; Brno University of Technology

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21561v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21561v1)

### 论文摘要

Large language models (LLMs) deployed as conversational agents are increasingly expected to follow procedural logic. While factual hallucination has received extensive attention, a different failure mode has been overlooked: procedural hallucination, in which an agent produces individually fluent and factually plausible utterances while deviating from the intended conversational process. We introduce DialFork, the first benchmark to measure this failure mode on raw dialogue text. DialFork is built by forking conformant dialogues at controlled turn positions and injecting one of four hallucination types, yielding 36,278 dialogues across 24 tasks in 12 domains; 30 human annotators validate the construction. We define three evaluation tasks that characterize procedural deviation at three granularities: how far a set of dialogues deviates from conformant ones (monitoring deployed agents), how far a single dialogue does (procedural retrieval), and at which turn a dialogue first goes off-track (real-time guardrails). For the first two, we propose dialog-flow-aware metrics, FlowKL and FlowScore, that compare dialogues by how they unfold rather than their content. At the dialogue level, FlowScore significantly outperforms all baselines across seven encoders, showing that semantic similarity is no substitute for procedural similarity. We release DialFork under the MIT license at [https://github.com/idiap/dialfork](https://github.com/idiap/dialfork), hoping the community will measure conversational process alongside content.

**论文简评**: 本文关注于大语言模型在对话过程中出现的“流程幻觉”问题，即模型虽然生成内容真实流畅，但对话流程却偏离了预期的操作逻辑。为此，作者提出了DIALFORK数据集，基于STAR任务，通过受控分叉生成大规模包含多类型流程幻觉的对话，并经30位标注者人工验证。论文设计了三类评测任务（集合、对话、回合粒度），并提出了面向对话流程的新型评测指标FLOWSCORE和FLOWKL。实验结果显示，这些流程感知指标能显著优于传统语义相似度方法，精确检测流程幻觉，推动了对话代理系统过程层面的评估与优化。

### From Research Goals to Scientific Closure: Evaluating Agents Through Evidence-Supported Physics Research

> Authors: Yijia Mao, Song Dai, Tao Chen, Xiangyu Lu, Linqian Zeng, Chenxi Hu, Sitong Zhou, Dongyang He, Xiaodan Mao, Linxuan Zhang, Tong Zhu, Yafu Li, Fangchen Yu, Peng Ye, Feng He, Yu Cheng, Yun Luo<br> Affiliations: Shanghai AI Laboratory; Shanghai Jiao Tong University; The HongKong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou); Nanyang Technological University

[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21767v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21767v1)

### 论文摘要

Can AI agents organize scientific investigations that lead to well-supported conclusions? To evaluate this capability, we introduce PhysClosureBench, a benchmark of 50 expert-verified, goal-specified research tasks spanning strong-field, attosecond, and neighboring physics areas. Each task specifies the research objective and essential scientific constraints, while leaving the agent to organize the investigation and determine how to generate and validate the evidence needed to answer the question. A 20-criterion Atomic Score measures local scientific accomplishment, while Research Closure (RC0–RC4) assesses how fully the investigation resolves the specified research question based on whether its indispensable evidence requirements and applicable scope conditions are satisfied. We evaluate seven frontier LLMs, where Claude-Opus-5 achieves the highest mean Atomic Score (6.29⁄10) but reaches RC4 in only 32.7% of attempts. Across the benchmark, 36.2% of attempts scoring at least 8⁄10 still fall short of RC4, while all seven models reach RC4 on substantially fewer tasks in all three runs than in at least one. Case-level analysis further illustrates how misinterpretation of generated results and unresolved physical applicability limits can lead to incomplete closure. Together, these results reveal a persistent gap between AI agents’ local scientific accomplishments and their ability to achieve complete research closure.

**论文简评**: 本论文关注现有AI代理在物理科研任务中是否具备从目标设定到科学结论闭环的能力。为此，作者提出了PhysClosureBench基准，涵盖50个由专家验证的物理研究任务，要求AI自主规划研究流程并产出有证据支撑的科学结论。评测框架包括细粒度的Atomic Score（20项科学完成度维度）与分层的Research Closure指标（RC0-RC4，衡量研究结论的完整性）。实验结果表明，尽管Claude-Opus-5等主流大模型在Atomic Score上表现较好（如6.29/10），但仅有32.7%的尝试实现完全闭环（RC4），且高分并不代表科学问题被真正解决，揭示出AI在实现科学研究闭环上仍有显著差距。
