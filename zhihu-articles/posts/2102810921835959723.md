**Theorem** *Blocked Layout* is a *Linear Layout*

**Proof**Consider a logical tensor with shape $(d_1, \cdots, d_l)$. Here $l$ is the rank of the tensor.

For each dimension $i\in\{1,\cdots,l\}$, let $r_i,t_i,w_i$ denote the number of registers, threads, and warps distributed along that dimension, respectively.

Assuming all these quantities are powers of two and the layout fully covers the logical tensor, we have

$$ r_i \times t_i \times w_i = d_i $$

For each dimension $i$, we define the hardware index:

$$ \text{reg}_i \in [0,r_i),\; \text{thread}_i \in [0,t_i), \; \text{warp}_i \in [0,w_i) $$

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{reg} &\triangleq (\text{reg}_1,\ldots,\text{reg}_l) \in \prod_{i=1}^{l}[0,r_i),\\ \text{thread} &\triangleq (\text{thread}_1,\ldots,\text{thread}_l)\in \prod_{i=1}^{l}[0,t_i),\\ \text{warp} &\triangleq (\text{warp}_1,\ldots, \text{warp}_l) \in \prod_{i=1}^{l}[0,w_i) \end{aligned} $$

**Constructive Definition of Blocked Layout**

We first define the domain and codomain of blocked layout

$$ L: \prod_{i=1}^l[0,r_i) \times \prod_{i=1}^l[0,t_i) \times \prod_{i=1}^l [0,w_i) \rightarrow (i_1, \cdots, i_l) $$

For each dimension, its corresponding output coordinate is

$$ L(\text{reg}, \text{thread}, \text{warp})_i \triangleq \text{reg}_i+r_i \times \text{thread}_i+ r_i \times t_i \times \text{warp}_i $$

Furthermore, we define a permutation of $\{1, \cdots, l\}$ as “order” $\Omega \in S_{l}$, where $\Omega_i$ is the $i$-th Fastest running dimension.

Thus, the corresponding coordinate can be written as

$$ L(\text{reg}, \text{thread}, \text{warp})_{\Omega_i} \triangleq \text{reg}_{i}+r_{i} \times \text{thread}_{i}+ r_{i} \times t_{i} \times \text{warp}_{i} $$

**Bit Representation**

We define their corresponding bit-widths as follows:

$$ R_i \triangleq \log_2(r_i),\;T_i \triangleq \log_2(t_i) ,\;W_i \triangleq \log_2(w_i) $$

$$ D_i \triangleq \textbf{Bitwidth}(d_i) = \log_2(d_i) $$

Then we have

$$ R_i + T_i + W_i = D_i $$

we consider the bit representation for register, thread and warp:

$$ \textbf{reg}_i \in \mathbb{F}_2^{R_i}, \;\textbf{thread}_i \in \mathbb{F}_2^{T_i}, \;\textbf{warp}_i \in \mathbb{F}_2^{W_i} $$

We then concatenate ($\|$) the bits across all tensor dimensions to obtain the global register, thread, and warp vectors:

$$ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{reg}&\triangleq\mathbf{reg}_1\|\cdots\|\mathbf{reg}_l,\\ \mathbf{thread}&\triangleq \mathbf{thread}_1\|\cdots\|\mathbf{thread}_l,\\ \mathbf{warp}&\triangleq \mathbf{warp}_1\|\cdots\|\mathbf{warp}_l, \end{aligned} $$

$$ R \triangleq \sum_{i=1}^lR_i, \; T \triangleq \sum_{i=1}^lT_i, W \triangleq \sum_{i=1}^lW_i $$

The **Blocked Layout** could be expressed as

$$ \mathbf{L}(\mathbf{reg}, \mathbf{thread}, \mathbf{warp})_{\Omega_i} \triangleq\mathbf{reg}_{i} \| \mathbf{thread}_{i} \| \mathbf{warp}_{i} $$

Furthermore we define a $\mathbf{P}$ to only express the concatenation (which is apparently linear):

$$ \begin{aligned} \mathbf{P}: \mathbb F_2^R\times \mathbb F_2^T\times \mathbb F_2^W &\longrightarrow \prod_{i=1}^l\mathbb F_2^{D_i}\\ \mathbf{P}(\mathbf{reg},\mathbf{thread},\mathbf{warp})_i &= \mathbf{reg}_{i} \| \mathbf{thread}_{i} \| \mathbf{warp}_{i} \end{aligned} $$

Then the $\mathbf{L}$ could be expressed as

$$ \mathbf{L} = \Omega^{-1} \circ \mathbf{P} $$

Since both $\Omega$ and $\mathbf{P}$ are linear, the $\mathbf{L}$ is also linear. One can also use the definition of linear mapping to prove it, i.e. proving

$$ \mathbf{L}(a \oplus b) = \mathbf{L}(a) \oplus \mathbf{L}(b) $$
