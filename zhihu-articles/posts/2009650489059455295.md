## The flavor of the bitter lesson for computer vision

Vincent Sitzmann

Feb 1, 2026

I believe that computer vision as we know it is about to go away.

我相信，我们如今所熟知的计算机视觉，即将走向终结。

Historically, we have treated vision as a mapping from images to intermediate representations—classes, segmentation masks, or 3D reconstructions. But in the era of the Bitter Lesson, these distinct tasks are becoming qualitatively no different than edge detection: historical artifacts of scoping “solvable intermediate problems” rather than solving intelligence.

长久以来，我们一直将视觉视为从图像到中间表示的映射——这里的中间表示包括类别标签、分割掩码或3D重建结果。但在《苦涩的教训》所揭示的时代趋势下，这些独立的任务在本质上已经和边缘检测没有区别：它们都是我们为了划定“可解的中间问题”、而非真正解决智能本身，而留下的历史遗留产物。

While the “LLM moment” in NLP clarified that language modeling is the ultimate objective, the vision community is still debating the flavor of its own revolution. We continue to fine-tune models for specific tasks like point tracking, segmentation, or 3D reconstruction—even as world models emerge, skirt all conventional intermediate representations, and directly solve a problem dramatically more general than everything our community has tackled in the past.

当自然语言处理（NLP）领域的“大语言模型（LLM）时刻”已经明确，语言建模本身就是最终目标时，计算机视觉社区仍在争论自身革命的具体形态。我们仍在为点跟踪、分割、3D重建等特定任务微调模型——即便世界模型已经出现，它们绕开了所有传统的中间表示，直接解决了一个比我们社区过去所有研究都要通用得多的问题。

In this post, I argue that the future of computer vision is as part of end-to-end perception-action loops. The historical boundaries between computer vision, robot learning, and control will dissolve. Frontier research will no longer draw a boundary between “seeing” and “learning to act.”

在这篇文章中，我提出：计算机视觉的未来，是成为端到端感知-动作循环的一部分。计算机视觉、机器人学习与控制之间的传统边界将会消解。前沿研究将不再为“看见”与“学习行动”划定界限。

As a special case, I will discuss the waning importance of 3D representations: I predict that just as we no longer hand-craft features for detection, we will soon stop using 3D as part of embodied intelligence.

作为一个特例，我将讨论3D表示正在衰减的重要性：我预测，就像我们如今不再为检测任务手工设计特征一样，我们很快也会停止在具身智能中使用3D表示。

How we arrived at today’s scope of computer vision

我们是如何走到如今的计算机视觉研究范畴的

To understand where the field is going, it is instructive to ask what vision actually is.

要理解这个领域的未来走向，我们首先要追问：视觉究竟是什么？

Historically, we have treated vision as the “visual perception” sub-module of intelligence—often summarized as “knowing what is where.” However, this is not a well-defined task. It does not specify a strictly falsifiable input-output behavior: The inputs are images or video, sure—but what are the outputs? Consequently, it does not lend itself to being definitively “solved.”

长久以来，我们一直将视觉视为智能的“视觉感知”子模块——通常被概括为“知道什么东西在什么地方”。然而，这并不是一个定义严谨的任务。它没有给出严格可证伪的输入-输出行为规范：输入确实是图像或视频，但输出究竟是什么？因此，这个任务根本无法被彻底“解决”。

In the real world, there is a much clearer metric for perception: intelligent action. An agent has succeeded at perceiving the world when it can map present and past percepts to actions that accomplish its goals, especially when exposed to new and unseen environments. This is easily falsifiable: I want to be able to demonstrate a task to my robot such as cleaning out the dishwasher, and I expect the robot to succeed at this task. If it succeeded, it clearly perceived what was important.

在现实世界中，感知有一个清晰得多的衡量标准：智能行动。当一个智能体能够将当前和过往的感知输入，映射为能够完成目标的动作时——尤其是在全新的、从未见过的环境中——它就成功完成了对世界的感知。这个标准是极易证伪的：我可以给我的机器人演示一个任务，比如清理洗碗机，然后我只需要看机器人能否成功完成这个任务。如果它成功了，就说明它显然感知到了所有重要的信息。

Why, then, did we not start there? In the past, learning perception-action loops directly was intractable. Because the role of a scientist is to work on the solvable, we split off computer vision. The community converged on a niche of building algorithms that map images to intermediate representations that appeared practically useful—classification, segmentation, optical flow, 3D reconstruction, and SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping).

那么，我们为什么没有从一开始就以此为目标？在过去，直接学习感知-动作循环是完全无法实现的。因为科学家的职责是研究可解的问题，我们便拆分出了计算机视觉这个领域。整个社区逐渐聚焦于一个细分方向：构建算法，将图像映射为看起来有实用价值的中间表示——包括分类、分割、光流、3D重建，以及同步定位与地图构建（SLAM）。

Simultaneously, robot learning and control were scoped as the study of algorithms that ingest these specific representations—point clouds, bounding boxes, and masks—and map them to actions.

与此同时，机器人学习与控制被划定为这样一个研究领域：构建算法，接收这些特定的表示（点云、边界框、掩码），并将其映射为动作。

This factorization was a necessary compromise for the time. However, I believe this “modular” model of embodied intelligence is quickly losing its raison d’être.

这种任务拆分在当时是必要的妥协。但我认为，这种具身智能的“模块化”范式，正迅速失去其存在的合理性。

Case study: How 3D may become obsolete for training embodied intelligence models

案例研究：3D表示为何会在具身智能模型的训练中被淘汰

Rich Sutton’s Bitter Lesson states: “General methods leveraging massive computation… consistently outperform human-crafted, task-specific systems, even though the latter feel clever initially.”

理查德·萨顿在《苦涩的教训》中提出：“利用海量计算的通用方法……始终会超越人工设计的、面向特定任务的系统，即便后者初看之下充满巧思。”

In computer vision, most researchers readily apply this lesson to algorithms, acknowledging that neural networks with physical inductive biases are rarely scalable. Yet, surprisingly few apply the same logic to representations.

在计算机视觉领域，大多数研究者都愿意将这个教训应用于算法层面，承认带有物理归纳偏置的神经网络往往不具备可扩展性。但令人意外的是，极少有人将同样的逻辑应用于表示层面。

Take the very notion of a 3D representation, be it a point cloud, a radiance field, a signed distance function, or a voxel grid. Consider the fundamental loop of embodied intelligence: perception in, action out. In a world where we can train end-to-end algorithms to tackle this behavior directly, hand-crafting an explicit intermediate representation like “3D structure” becomes exactly the kind of clever, human-designed bottleneck that the Bitter Lesson warns against.

就拿3D表示这个概念本身来说，无论是点云、辐射场、符号距离函数还是体素网格，都不例外。我们不妨看看具身智能的核心循环：感知输入，动作输出。在我们已经能够训练端到端算法直接实现这个循环的当下，人工设计一个像“3D结构”这样的显式中间表示，恰恰就是《苦涩的教训》所警示的那种、充满人工巧思却会成为瓶颈的设计。

To see why, try a thought experiment. Look at the room you are currently sitting in. If I gave you a perfect 3D reconstruction of this scene—a NeRF (Neural Radiance Field), a point cloud, whatever you choose—what real problem would you be able to solve with it?

要理解这一点，不妨做一个思想实验。看看你现在所处的房间。如果我给你这个场景的完美3D重建结果——无论是神经辐射场（NeRF）、点云，还是任何你想要的形式——你能用它解决什么实际问题？

There are of course niche applications such as novel view synthesis. But for any task involving embodied intelligence, you still need a separate, intelligent algorithm to ingest that 3D representation and decide what to do. The overall input-output behavior remains images to actions, reducing the 3D reconstruction to a clever pre-processing step. In the long arc of embodied intelligence, this factorization will not pass the test of time.

当然，它在新视角合成等细分领域有应用价值。但对于任何涉及具身智能的任务，你仍然需要一个独立的智能算法来接收这个3D表示，并决定要执行什么动作。整个系统的输入-输出逻辑依然是从图像到动作，3D重建最终只是一个充满巧思的预处理步骤。在具身智能的长期发展中，这种拆分方式终将被时代淘汰。

In fact, many tasks traditionally thought to rely on 3D are already being solved better by end-to-end learning. Take novel view synthesis: the state-of-the-art in few-shot view synthesis has for a while now not employed 3D differentiable rendering, but lies with generative world models. When my students Boyuan and Kiwhan developed History-Guided Video Diffusion, they generated novel views of RealEstate10k that looked far better than any 3D-structured algorithm I had ever worked on—and they did so almost as an afterthought.

事实上，很多传统上被认为必须依赖3D的任务，已经能通过端到端学习得到更好的解决。以新视角合成为例：少样本新视角合成的当前最优方法，早已不再使用3D可微渲染，而是基于生成式世界模型。我的学生Boyuan和Kiwhan在开发《历史引导的视频扩散模型》（History-Guided Video Diffusion）时，生成的RealEstate10k数据集新视角结果，比我接触过的所有基于3D结构的算法都要好得多——而这几乎只是他们顺手完成的附加成果。

SE(3) Camera Poses Will Go, Too

SE(3)相机位姿也将被淘汰

You might argue that these generative models are still conditioned on camera poses, obtained via conventional multi-view geometry (COLMAP) or learned equivalents. However, I predict that just like 3D representations, algorithms that output camera poses will also become obsolete. My lab has already shown that novel view synthesis can be formalized purely as a representation learning problem—without any concepts from multi-view geometry. No poses, no 3D.

你可能会反驳说，这些生成模型仍然依赖相机位姿作为条件，而这些位姿要么来自传统的多视图几何工具（如COLMAP），要么来自对应的学习方法。但我预测，就像3D表示一样，输出相机位姿的算法也终将被淘汰。我的实验室已经证明，新视角合成可以完全被形式化为一个表示学习问题——不需要任何多视图几何的概念，不需要位姿，也不需要3D。

Ego-motion (and therefore camera pose) is simply the most basic action an agent can take. It is not special. Ultimately, we must solve the problem of an AI controlling a body it has never inhabited before. In that context, inferring ego-motion is trivial compared to the complex control problems a general-purpose agent must solve. Whatever algorithm we converge on will handle ego-motion implicitly, without needing us to bake it in.

自运动（以及对应的相机位姿），只是智能体能够执行的最基础的动作，它并没有任何特殊性。最终，我们必须解决这样一个问题：让AI控制一个它从未使用过的身体。在这个场景下，相比于通用智能体必须解决的复杂控制问题，推断自运动只是一个微不足道的子问题。我们最终找到的通用算法，会隐式地处理自运动，根本不需要我们将其人工嵌入到系统中。

To get models competent at 3D editing, don’t train them for 3D editing

要得到擅长3D编辑的模型，不要为3D编辑任务训练它们

What about engineering tasks such as architecture, CAD, or manufacturing? Surely, we need explicit 3D representations to build a house or 3D-print an engine part. I agree that for the human-machine interface, having a 3D mesh representation and a CAD-like editor may be reasonable. However, my argument is not about how we talk to the machine, but how we train models that will ultimately help us automate 3D design tasks.

那建筑、CAD、制造业等工程任务呢？我们要建造房屋、3D打印发动机零件，显然需要显式的3D表示。我认同，对于人机交互界面来说，提供3D网格表示和类CAD的编辑器是合理的。但我的核心论点，无关我们如何与机器交互，而关乎我们如何训练那些最终帮我们实现3D设计任务自动化的模型。

Here, again, to obtain models that are maximally competent at assisting with the manipulation of both physical and digital 3D objects, we should not train them to explicitly produce expert-crafted 3D representations, nor bake such representations into their architectures. Instead, our goal should be to train general-purpose physical intelligence models directly on raw data, allowing them to learn their own internal, task-relevant structure. These internal representations need not—and likely will not—correspond to any human-designed notion of 3D blocking, meshing, or reconstruction. Only after such a model has been trained should it be fine-tuned to interface with whatever representation or toolchain we humans like to use.

在这里，同样的逻辑依然成立：要得到能够最大程度辅助我们操作物理和数字3D物体的模型，我们不应该训练它们显式地生成专家设计的3D表示，也不应该将这类表示嵌入到模型架构中。相反，我们的目标应该是，直接基于原始数据训练通用的物理智能模型，让它们学习自己的、与任务相关的内部结构。这些内部表示不需要——而且极大概率不会——对应任何人类设计的3D分块、网格或重建概念。只有当这类模型训练完成后，我们才应该对其进行微调，让它适配我们人类习惯使用的任何表示或工具链。

As for the final step—manufacturing the artifact—in the near term, we will similarly fine-tune models on 3D printer APIs or mesh file formats. In the very long run, I note that a 3D printer or excavator is essentially a robot: A physical machine that we seek to automate via AI. Hence, I find it plausible that we will eventually solve these challenges of 3D manufacturing in the same way in which we will solve embodied intelligence more generally, by yielding direct control of the actuators of the machine to the AI.

至于最终的步骤——制造产品——短期内，我们同样会基于3D打印机API或网格文件格式对模型进行微调。而从更长远的视角来看，3D打印机或挖掘机本质上就是一个机器人：一个我们希望通过AI实现自动化的物理机器。因此我认为，我们最终会以解决通用具身智能问题的方式，解决3D制造的这些挑战——也就是将机器执行器的直接控制权交给AI。

The key challenge of perception-action loops and world models

感知-动作循环与世界模型的核心挑战

The core challenge of embodied intelligence is the lack of paired perception–action data at scale. Deploying large numbers of robots in the real world is extremely expensive, and even if we could do so, it remains unclear what we would have them do. Collecting valuable data requires agents to perform meaningful, diverse behaviors. Today, this is largely achieved through teleoperation. This works remarkably well for self-driving—humans already drive cars, but it scales far less naturally to humanoid robots with dexterous hands.

具身智能的核心挑战，是缺乏大规模的成对感知-动作数据。在现实世界中部署大量机器人的成本极高，即便我们有能力做到，也依然不清楚该让它们做什么。收集有价值的数据，需要智能体执行有意义的、多样化的行为。如今，这主要通过远程遥操作实现。这种方式在自动驾驶领域效果极佳——因为人类本来就会开车，但对于拥有灵巧双手的人形机器人来说，它的可扩展性要差得多。

The long-term goal is robots that collect data autonomously, driven by intrinsic motivation—much like toddlers. While this concept of “intrinsic reward” has a rich history in the reinforcement learning (RL) community, current algorithms are far too sample-inefficient to be deployed on real robots. Moreover, unleashing large numbers of agents with essentially random policies into the physical world, where they can hurt themselvs and others, is simply not viable.

我们的长期目标，是让机器人像幼儿一样，在内在动机的驱动下自主收集数据。尽管“内在奖励”这个概念在强化学习（RL）领域已有悠久的研究历史，但当前的算法样本效率极低，根本无法部署在真实机器人上。此外，将大量本质上执行随机策略的智能体投放到现实世界中，可能会对它们自身和人类造成伤害，这显然是不可行的。

This, then, is the central question facing embodied intelligence today: how do we move toward closing the perception–action loop without having direct access to large-scale action data?

这就是如今具身智能领域面临的核心问题：在无法直接获取大规模动作数据的情况下，我们该如何实现闭环的感知-动作循环？

This is where world models enter the picture. On the surface, they may appear to be just another intermediate task—learned simulators that do not themselves address the core challenge. And indeed, on their own, they do not.

这正是世界模型的价值所在。表面上看，它们似乎只是又一个中间任务——只是学到的模拟器，本身并不能解决核心挑战。事实上，单靠世界模型确实无法解决。

They do, however, offer two promising angles.

但它们提供了两个极具潜力的解决方向。

First, video (and potentially audio) generative modeling provides a clearly scalable pre-training objective. Crucially, video does not merely capture raw sensory data—it also implicitly encodes a vast amount of information about not only physics and how the world works, but also human knowledge about skills, tasks, and their structure. Training a finite neural network to approximate this complex process could lead to useful representations that could hopefully be used as a basis to be fine-tuned into policies with useful know-how. However, this remains speculative: to date, I am not aware of any clear demonstration that video models can easily be fine-tuned into policies, though there are some early signs of life.

第一，视频（未来可能包括音频）生成建模提供了一个明确具备可扩展性的预训练目标。至关重要的是，视频不仅仅记录了原始的感官数据——它还隐式编码了海量信息，不仅包括物理规律和世界的运行方式，还包括人类关于技能、任务及其结构的知识。训练一个有限规模的神经网络来拟合这个复杂的过程，能够得到有用的表示，有望作为基础微调得到具备实用技能的策略。不过，这一点目前仍停留在推测阶段：尽管已经出现了一些早期的积极信号，但迄今为止，我还没有看到任何明确的证据，能够证明视频模型可以被轻松微调为策略。

Second, by extending video models to be action-conditional, they can serve as simulators in which agents can be trained. In principle, this enables a form of data amplification: expensive real-world interactions can be used to bootstrap a model that supports much richer virtual experience. At the same time, this approach exposes a fundamental chicken-and-egg problem. Training interactive world models requires paired action–observation data, which is precisely the resource we lack-excepting, again, self-driving, where such data is plenty. Unsurprisingly, existing systems exhibit only limited forms of interactivity, often reminiscent of video games, which likely made up a significant part of their training data.

第二，通过将视频模型扩展为动作条件式模型，它们可以作为模拟器，用于训练智能体。原则上，这能够实现一种数据扩增：我们可以用成本高昂的现实世界交互，来引导训练一个能够生成更丰富虚拟体验的模型。但与此同时，这种方法也暴露了一个根本性的“先有鸡还是先有蛋”的问题。训练交互式世界模型需要成对的动作-观测数据，而这恰恰是我们所缺乏的资源——再次排除自动驾驶领域，因为该领域有充足的这类数据。不出所料，现有的系统只具备有限的交互能力，往往和电子游戏类似，而电子游戏很可能是它们训练数据的重要组成部分。

For these reasons, I do not believe that video generative models will solve embodied intelligence. They may not even be a necessary component of the final solution. Rather, they should be seen as one of several early attempts at identifying a scalable pre-training objective for perception–action learning. At present, however, there is no clear answer to what the “right” pre-training task should be. The same is true for many of the other ingredients required to close the perception–action loop. Questions of intrinsic motivation, exploration, long-horizon memory, continual learning, and real-time control with large models remain wide open.

基于这些原因，我并不认为视频生成模型能够解决具身智能问题。它们甚至可能不是最终解决方案的必要组成部分。相反，它们应该被视为我们为感知-动作学习寻找可扩展预训练目标的诸多早期尝试之一。不过目前，对于什么才是“正确”的预训练任务，我们还没有明确的答案。对于实现感知-动作闭环所需的诸多其他要素，也是如此。内在动机、探索、长时序记忆、持续学习，以及大模型的实时控制等问题，仍然完全处于开放研究状态。

However, nevertheless, things have changed: I believe that we are now at a time where tackling these questions head-on is viable. This, then, is the central point of this article: A call to abandon the conventional boundaries between computer vision and robot learning, and instead ponder the problems that arise when we seek to build machines that both perceive and act.

但无论如何，局面已经改变：我相信，如今我们已经有能力直面这些问题。这正是本文的核心观点：呼吁大家摒弃计算机视觉与机器人学习之间的传统边界，转而聚焦于我们在构建既能感知、又能行动的机器时，所面临的真正问题。

## **The Bitter Lesson**

Rich Sutton

March 13, 2019

The biggest lesson that can be read from 70 years of AI research is that general methods that leverage computation are ultimately the most effective, and by a large margin. The ultimate reason for this is Moore's law, or rather its generalization of continued exponentially falling cost per unit of computation.

从70年的人工智能研究史中，我们能得到的最深刻的教训是：利用计算能力的通用方法，最终会以极大的优势成为最有效的方法。这背后的根本原因是摩尔定律，或者更准确地说，是单位计算成本持续呈指数级下降的普遍规律。

Most AI research has been conducted as if the computation available to the agent were constant (in which case leveraging human knowledge would be one of the only ways to improve performance) but, over a slightly longer time than a typical research project, massively more computation inevitably becomes available. Seeking an improvement that makes a difference in the shorter term, researchers seek to leverage their human knowledge of the domain, but the only thing that matters in the long run is the leveraging of computation.

大多数人工智能研究的开展，都基于一个隐含假设：智能体可用的计算资源是恒定的（在这种情况下，利用人类知识几乎是提升性能的唯一途径）。但只要时间跨度超过一个典型研究项目的周期，可用的计算资源必然会迎来爆发式增长。研究者们往往为了在短期内获得性能提升，试图利用自己在对应领域的人类知识，但从长远来看，唯一真正重要的，是对计算能力的利用。

These two need not run counter to each other, but in practice they tend to. Time spent on one is time not spent on the other. There are psychological commitments to investment in one approach or the other. And the human-knowledge approach tends to complicate methods in ways that make them less suited to taking advantage of general methods leveraging computation.

这两者并非必然对立，但在实践中往往会产生冲突。在一种方法上投入的时间，无法再用于另一种。研究者们会在心理上对自己投入的方法产生路径依赖。而基于人类知识的方法，往往会让系统变得复杂，使其不再适合适配那些利用计算能力的通用方法。

There were many examples of AI researchers' belated learning of this bitter lesson, and it is instructive to review some of the most prominent.

人工智能研究者们总是很晚才领悟到这个苦涩的教训，相关的案例不胜枚举，回顾其中最具代表性的几个，会给我们带来极大的启发。

In computer chess, the methods that defeated the world champion, Kasparov, in 1997, were based on massive, deep search. At the time, this was looked upon with dismay by the majority of computer-chess researchers who had pursued methods that leveraged human understanding of the special structure of chess.

在计算机国际象棋领域，1997年击败世界冠军卡斯帕罗夫的方法，基于大规模的深度搜索。当时，大多数计算机国际象棋研究者对此感到沮丧与不满，他们此前一直致力于利用人类对国际象棋特殊结构的理解来设计方法。

When a simpler, search-based approach with special hardware and software proved vastly more effective, these human-knowledge-based chess researchers were not good losers. They said that ``brute force" search may have won this time, but it was not a general strategy, and anyway it was not how people played chess.

当这种基于搜索、搭配专用软硬件的更简单的方法，被证明效果远超人工设计的方法时，这些基于人类知识的象棋研究者们并没有坦然接受失败。他们说，“暴力”搜索也许这次赢了，但它不是通用的策略，而且无论如何，人类下棋根本不是这样的。

These researchers wanted methods based on human input to win and were disappointed when they did not.

这些研究者希望基于人类输入的方法能够获胜，当结果事与愿违时，他们便感到了失望。

A similar pattern of research progress was seen in computer Go, only delayed by a further 20 years. Enormous initial efforts went into avoiding search by taking advantage of human knowledge, or of the special features of the game, but all those efforts proved irrelevant, or worse, once search was applied effectively at scale.

计算机围棋领域的研究进展，呈现出了完全相同的模式，只是晚了20年。研究者们最初投入了巨大的精力，试图利用人类知识或围棋的特殊特征来避免搜索，但当大规模搜索被有效应用后，所有这些努力都被证明毫无价值，甚至起到了反作用。

Also important was the use of learning by self play to learn a value function (as it was in many other games and even in chess, although learning did not play a big role in the 1997 program that first beat a world champion).

同样关键的，是通过自博弈学习价值函数的方法（这一点在很多其他游戏中，甚至国际象棋中都成立，尽管在1997年首个击败世界冠军的程序中，学习并没有扮演核心角色）。

Learning by self play, and learning in general, is like search in that it enables massive computation to be brought to bear. Search and learning are the two most important classes of techniques for utilizing massive amounts of computation in AI research.

自博弈学习，以及广义的学习，和搜索一样，都能够让海量的计算能力发挥作用。搜索与学习，是人工智能研究中利用大规模计算能力的两类最重要的技术。

In computer Go, as in computer chess, researchers' initial effort was directed towards utilizing human understanding (so that less search was needed) and only much later was much greater success had by embracing search and learning.

和计算机国际象棋一样，在计算机围棋领域，研究者们最初的努力都集中在利用人类的理解（从而减少所需的搜索量），直到很久之后，他们拥抱搜索与学习，才取得了巨大的成功。

In speech recognition, there was an early competition, sponsored by DARPA, in the 1970s. Entrants included a host of special methods that took advantage of human knowledge---knowledge of words, of phonemes, of the human vocal tract, etc. On the other side were newer methods that were more statistical in nature and did much more computation, based on hidden Markov models (HMMs).

在语音识别领域，1970年代曾有一场由DARPA赞助的早期竞赛。参赛者包括大量利用人类知识的特殊方法——关于单词、音素、人类声道的知识等等。而另一边，是更新的、本质上更偏向统计的方法，它们基于隐马尔可夫模型（HMMs），进行了多得多的计算。

Again, the statistical methods won out over the human-knowledge-based methods. This led to a major change in all of natural language processing, gradually over decades, where statistics and computation came to dominate the field.

结果再一次，统计方法战胜了基于人类知识的方法。这引发了整个自然语言处理领域的重大变革，在数十年的时间里，统计与计算逐渐主导了这个领域。

The recent rise of deep learning in speech recognition is the most recent step in this consistent direction. Deep learning methods rely even less on human knowledge, and use even more computation, together with learning on huge training sets, to produce dramatically better speech recognition systems.

近年来深度学习在语音识别领域的崛起，正是这个持续趋势的最新一步。深度学习方法对人类知识的依赖更低，对计算能力的利用更多，再加上在海量训练集上的学习，最终构建出了性能实现飞跃的语音识别系统。

As in the games, researchers always tried to make systems that worked the way the researchers thought their own minds worked---they tried to put that knowledge in their systems---but it proved ultimately counterproductive, and a colossal waste of researcher's time, when, through Moore's law, massive computation became available and a means was found to put it to good use.

和游戏领域一样，研究者们总是试图构建符合自己对人类思维方式理解的系统——他们试图把自己的知识植入系统中。但最终，当摩尔定律带来了海量的可用计算能力，当我们找到了有效利用这些计算能力的方法时，这种做法被证明最终只会适得其反，更是对研究者时间的巨大浪费。

In computer vision, there has been a similar pattern. Early methods conceived of vision as searching for edges, or generalized cylinders, or in terms of SIFT (Scale-Invariant Feature Transform) features. But today all this is discarded. Modern deep-learning neural networks use only the notions of convolution and certain kinds of invariances, and perform much better.

计算机视觉领域也呈现出了完全相同的模式。早期的方法将视觉视为边缘检测、广义圆柱体搜索，或是基于尺度不变特征变换（SIFT）特征的匹配。但如今，所有这些方法都被抛弃了。现代深度学习神经网络只使用卷积和某些不变性的概念，就能实现好得多的性能。

This is a big lesson. As a field, we still have not thoroughly learned it, as we are continuing to make the same kind of mistakes. To see this, and to effectively resist it, we have to understand the appeal of these mistakes. We have to learn the bitter lesson that building in how we think we think does not work in the long run.

这是一个深刻的教训。作为一个领域，我们至今仍未彻底领悟它，因为我们还在不断犯着同样的错误。要认清这一点，并有效避免重蹈覆辙，我们必须理解这些错误的吸引力所在。我们必须吸取这个苦涩的教训：从长远来看，把我们自认为的思维方式植入系统，是行不通的。

The bitter lesson is based on the historical observations that 1) AI researchers have often tried to build knowledge into their agents, 2) this always helps in the short term, and is personally satisfying to the researcher, but 3) in the long run it plateaus and even inhibits further progress, and 4) breakthrough progress eventually arrives by an opposing approach based on scaling computation by search and learning.

这个苦涩的教训基于以下历史观察：1）人工智能研究者总是试图将知识植入智能体中；2）这种做法在短期内总能带来提升，也能给研究者带来个人成就感；3）但从长远来看，它会让性能进入瓶颈，甚至阻碍进一步的发展；4）最终的突破性进展，总是来自与之相反的方法——基于搜索和学习，对计算能力进行规模化利用。

The eventual success is tinged with bitterness, and often incompletely digested, because it is success over a favored, human-centric approach.

这种最终的成功总是带着苦涩，也往往无法被彻底接受，因为它战胜的是一种我们偏爱的、以人为中心的方法。

One thing that should be learned from the bitter lesson is the great power of general purpose methods, of methods that continue to scale with increased computation even as the available computation becomes very great. The two methods that seem to scale arbitrarily in this way are search and learning.

我们从这个苦涩的教训中应该学到的第一点，是通用方法的巨大力量——这类方法能够随着计算能力的提升持续扩展，即便可用的计算资源已经极为庞大。而能够以这种方式无限扩展的两类方法，正是搜索与学习。

The second general point to be learned from the bitter lesson is that the actual contents of minds are tremendously, irredeemably complex; we should stop trying to find simple ways to think about the contents of minds, such as simple ways to think about space, objects, multiple agents, or symmetries.

我们应该学到的第二点，是心智的实际内容是极其、且不可挽回地复杂的；我们应该停止试图用简单的方式去理解心智的内容，比如用简单的方式去理解空间、物体、多智能体或是对称性。

All these are part of the arbitrary, intrinsically-complex, outside world. They are not what should be built in, as their complexity is endless; instead we should build in only the meta-methods that can find and capture this arbitrary complexity.

所有这些，都是那个随机的、本质复杂的外部世界的一部分。我们不应该把它们植入系统中，因为它们的复杂性是无穷无尽的；相反，我们应该只植入那些能够发现和捕捉这种任意复杂性的元方法。

Essential to these methods is that they can find good approximations, but the search for them should be by our methods, not by us. We want AI agents that can discover like we can, not which contain what we have discovered. Building in our discoveries only makes it harder to see how the discovering process can be done.

这些方法的核心，是它们能够找到好的近似解，但寻找近似解的过程，应该由我们的方法完成，而不是由我们自己完成。我们想要的，是能够像我们一样去发现的人工智能，而不是包含了我们已经发现的内容的人工智能。把我们的发现植入系统，只会让我们更难看清发现的过程本身该如何实现。
