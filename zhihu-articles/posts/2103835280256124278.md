**Theorem***MMA Layout* is also a *Linear Layout*.

Consider a matrix multiply and accumulate operation `D = A * B + D`. Here we take the `wgmma` register fragment for `A` as an instance. The other cases, such as the register fragment for `D`, as well as Turing/Ampere `mma.sync` are similar. Shared Memory Matrix Layouts, which correspond to `SharedEncoding` in Triton, are out of scope. Note that Triton currently does not support Volta Tensor Cores.

It is easy to construct the mapping between hardware indices and logical tensor coordinates...

**Constructive Definition of MMA Layout**

We first define the element size: $$ \text{element_size}\in\{32,16,8,1\} $$ For convenience, define $$ q\triangleq\frac{32}{\text{element_size}} $$ The hardware indices are defined as follows: $$ \text{reg}\in[0,4q), \text{thread}\in[0,32), \text{warp}\in[0,4) $$ The logical tensor coordinates $(i,j)$ are then defined as:

$$ i =16\times\text{warp} +\left\lfloor\frac{\text{thread}}{4}\right\rfloor +8 \times \left(\left\lfloor\frac{\text{reg}}{q}\right\rfloor\bmod 2\right) $$

$$ j= q\times (\text{thread}\bmod 4) +4q \times \left\lfloor\frac{\text{reg}}{2q}\right\rfloor +(\text{reg}\bmod q) $$

Finally, we define the MMA layout as the mapping from hardware indices to logical tensor coordinates:

$$ L(\text{reg},\text{thread},\text{warp}) \triangleq(i,j) \in [0, 64) \times [0,2^{s + 3}) $$ **Bit Representation**<br><br>Let $s=\log_2 q\in\{0,1,2,5\}$, write the hardware indices as bit vectors:

$$ \mathbf{reg}=(r_0,\dots,r_{s+1})\in\mathbb F_2^{s+2},\quad \mathbf{thread}=(t_0,\dots,t_4)\in\mathbb F_2^{5},\quad \mathbf{warp}=(w_0,w_1)\in\mathbb F_2^{2} $$

Then

$$ \mathbf{i}=(\,t_2,\,t_3,\,t_4,\,r_s,\,w_0,\,w_1\,)\in\mathbb F_2^{6} $$

$$ \mathbf{j}=(\,r_0,\dots,r_{s-1},\,t_0,\,t_1,\,r_{s+1}\,)\in\mathbb F_2^{s+3} $$

$$ \mathbf{L}(\mathbf{reg}, \mathbf{thread}, \mathbf{warp}) \triangleq(\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{j}) \in \mathbb F_2^{6} \times \mathbb F_2^{s+3} $$

Therefore $\mathbf{L}$ is a permutation, then a linear mapping.
