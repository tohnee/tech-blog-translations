From personal stories and cinematic short films to innovative brand content, people have been crafting impressive clips in our AI filmmaking tool [Flow](https://labs.google/flow/about) — adding up to 100 million videos created since launching in May.

To inspire more creativity, we’re doubling the AI credits you get as a Google AI Ultra subscriber, bringing your monthly allocation from 12,500 to 25,000. This means you'll be able to generate even more clips and scenes as you build your story in Flow.

These credits can also be used in [Whisk](https://labs.google/fx/tools/whisk), our experiment that allows you to create images using both text and image prompts. Whisk is expanding to [77 more countries](https://labs.google/fx/tools/whisk/faq) starting tomorrow. Whether you’re using Whisk to express yourself or working on a film with Flow, you’ll be able to create even more.

Existing AI Ultra subscribers will see the increase the first time their plan renews. Workspace users will see the increase over the [next few days](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2025/08/increased-credit-limit-ai-ultra-business-whisk-countries.html).
