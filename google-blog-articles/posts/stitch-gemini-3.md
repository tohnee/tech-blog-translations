Starting today, we’re bringing the power of Gemini 3 to [Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/), our experimental AI-powered design tool. Gemini 3 in Stitch will mean higher quality UI generation to help you bring any idea to life.

We’re also introducing a new feature called "Prototypes." True to the name, you can now "stitch" together different screens on your canvas to create a working prototype. Building on static screens, you can now design interactions and entire user flows.

This feature is experimental and we have a lot of improvements coming, so let us know what you think.

Get started today at [stitch.withgoogle.com](http://stitch.withgoogle.com/).
