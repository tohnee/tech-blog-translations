For years, we’ve seen how AI can inspire new ideas and help create top-notch images and videos. Today, we’re bringing those capabilities to more people by rolling out [Pomelli](https://labs.google.com/pomelli/about/) in English across the E.U., Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland and the U.K.

Pomelli, an experiment from [Google Labs](https://labs.google/), built in partnership with [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/), is an AI-powered marketing tool designed to help small businesses easily generate on-brand content, like product images and social media campaigns.

The tool uses AI to understand your business and create tailored content in three steps:

- **Analyze**: Pomelli scans your website to understand your business’s identity, including your brand’s unique voice, fonts and colors.
- **Ideate**: Using that identity, it suggests custom campaign ideas, or you can collaborate using your own prompt.
- **Create**: Pomelli generates high-quality assets for social media, web and ads that you can edit and download directly within the tool.

[Pomelli](https://support.google.com/labs/answer/16715058) empowers small businesses with the best technology so they can create high-quality, on-brand content.
