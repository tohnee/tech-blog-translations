How are advancements like Genie 3 and improved benchmarking helping shape the future of AI?

In the latest episode of the Google AI: Release Notes podcast, host Logan Kilpatrick sits down with Google DeepMind CEO [Demis Hassabis](https://blog.google/authors/demis-hassabis/) to discuss [Deep Think](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-2-5-deep-think/) in Gemini 2.5, the "world model" capabilities of [Genie 3](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/) and the new [Game Arena on Kaggle](https://blog.google/technology/ai/kaggle-game-arena/) as a benchmark for driving the industry closer to artificial general intelligence (AGI).
