The latest episode of the Google AI: Release Notes podcast focuses on long context in Gemini — meaning how much information our AI models can process as input at once — and how that impacts its ability to answer questions and complete tasks.

Host Logan Kilpatrick talks to Nikolay Savinov, a research scientist at Google DeepMind, about the challenges and future of long context, including its importance in coding and agents.
