For small to medium-sized businesses, keeping up with the demands of digital marketing can be a full-time job that they don’t have the time or money to afford. With Pomelli, a free tool from Google Labs, we want to help these businesses grow by making it easier to generate on-brand photos and marketing campaigns that feel authentic and professional.

Today we’re introducing Photoshoot, a new feature in [Pomelli](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/pomelli/) that uses business context (Business DNA) and [Nano Banana](https://gemini.google/overview/image-generation/) image generation to turn product images into a professional-grade studio shot.

## Generate professional shots, for free, in a few clicks

Whether you're selling handmade jewelry, artisanal coffee or promoting a yoga studio, high-quality visuals are essential for building trust with customers. With Photoshoot, you can generate stunning product images for your website and social content.

Here’s how it works:

- **Pick a product:** Start with any picture, and don’t worry about polish — we’ll take care of it
- **Choose a template:** Select from professionally curated templates like studio or lifestyle, or let Pomelli suggest some for you
- **Generate:** We automatically apply your business’ aesthetics to generate professional-looking images that feel on brand
- **Refine:** Edit and adjust your images with finishing touches

All images can be downloaded or added to your Business DNA to use later, like when you’re creating a campaign.

### **Improved image generation**

In addition to Photoshoot, we’re introducing a new design to express your ideas both visually and in words:

- Improved image models with more accuracy, following your prompt
- Editing capabilities, e.g. "change my background to a forest"
- Style reference image, e.g. restyle <insert image> to look like <insert image> style

### **More specific campaigns**

We’re improving campaign accuracy by giving you two options:

- Upload images to campaign prompt to ground your creatives
- Add product context by entering your product URL to create campaigns based on the images, title and description from your site. You could type a prompt like, “create a promo for my new necklaces <insert URL>.”

These will help you create very specific campaigns to help your business grow.
