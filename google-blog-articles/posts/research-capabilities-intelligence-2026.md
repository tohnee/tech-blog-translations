Technology is supercharging scientific discovery and improving people's lives. At Google Research, we’re pioneering breakthroughs across domains and bringing our research to reality to benefit people everywhere.

Today at Research@ in Mountain View, we’re sharing our [latest milestones](https://research.google/blog/research-2026-10-ways-were-amplifying-human-ingenuity/) and some of the advanced AI capabilities and intelligence that we’re putting in the hands of experts. Our latest breakthroughs help drive progress on the world’s most pressing challenges including healthcare, crisis resilience, and beyond. This momentum builds on our recent announcements from [AMIE](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/health/amie-clinical-study-lancet) in the clinic, to [Earth AI](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/health/google-earth-ai/) for public health.
