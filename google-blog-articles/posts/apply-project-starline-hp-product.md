Earlier this year, we announced [Project Starline is coming out of the lab and into the world](https://blog.google/technology/research/google-project-starline-hp-partnership/), with a focus on transforming the way distributed teams and individuals connect in the workplace. Through our partnership with HP, our technology will be commercially available starting in 2025.

Today, we're sharing a first look at the product design, built in collaboration with HP. The device's design blends into meeting rooms, letting the experience take over so you can focus on what matters most: the other person. This helps make communication as natural as if two people were across from each other in the same room.

## Apply now to receive the Project Starline x HP product at launch [1](#footnote-1)

We’ve seen incredible interest from companies eager to bring Project Starline to their employees. Starting today, [companies can now apply](https://reinvent.hp.com/next-gen-collaboration) to be among the first to receive the product when it launches in 2025. Through this program with HP, companies can sign up to secure their place in line and gain access to exclusive product information and updates.

Learn more by visiting [starline.google](http://starline.google/).
