NotebookLM is a tool for understanding anything. We [recently announced](https://workspace.google.com/blog/product-announcements/empowering-businesses-with-AI) that we’re making [NotebookLM Plus](https://notebooklm.google/plus) — our premium version — part of more Google Workspace plans, giving tens of millions of Workspace customers benefits like more than 5x usage limits and features like shared team notebooks and the ability to customize how NotebookLM responds. Here are five ways to get started with NotebookLM Plus for your business.

## 1. Access a central hub for your project or team.

NotebookLM lets you upload and organize all types of sources — documents, PDFs, Google Drive files, videos, audio recordings and more — in one place. This flexibility lets your team access the information they need and find connections, no matter the format. NotebookLM breaks down silos and connects diverse sources so it’s easier to get things done.

Upload PDFs, YouTube videos, websites, and more sources into a single notebook to summarize and find connections.

![Various types of sources, including text, pdf, presentation and video shown as sources uploaded to NotebookLM](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/NotebookLMBlogPDF.width-1200.format-webp.webp)

## 2. Gain insights in one click.

Skip the hassle of sifting through endless text. Using Gemini 2.0, NotebookLM can quickly analyze data, deliver concise summaries and extract key insights. Whether it's market trends or competitor analysis, NotebookLM gives you the information you need to make informed decisions — fast.

Create pre-defined outputs with a single click or generate Audio Overviews to listen on the go.

## 3. Customize chats for your team.

NotebookLM Plus lets you tailor chat responses to meet your team's unique needs. Personalize how information is presented, whether you need insights delivered in the style of an analyst, a marketer or a strategist. From generating detailed market reports to summarizing complex research, NotebookLM Plus adapts to what you need, ensuring every response aligns with your team's goals.

Customize the response style with NotebookLM Plus.

## 4. Streamline onboarding and team collaboration.

NotebookLM Plus makes it easy to create shared notebooks for your team. These centralized resources can be used to streamline onboarding, so new employees get quick access to essential information from internal documents and external sources. Whether it's training materials, company FAQs or key processes, NotebookLM Plus helps your team stay aligned and informed.

Share your notebook with your team and add a customized welcome note.

## 5. Make learning fun with Audio Overviews.

Getting up to speed on a new topic can be time consuming and overwhelming, but NotebookLM changes that. With Audio Overviews, you can create podcast-style conversations with two AI hosts that highlight key takeaways from your documents. This engaging format makes learning easier and more enjoyable. And, with the new interactive mode, you can ask follow-up questions and go back and forth as you deep dive further into the material.

Join an Audio Overview and ask questions with the new interactive mode.

NotebookLM Plus is now available in all [Workspace Business and Enterprise Standard plans and above](https://workspace.google.com/pricing.html?source=gafb-home-globalnav-en), can be purchased standalone via [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/resources/notebooklm-enterprise) and is coming soon as part of [Google One AI Premium](https://one.google.com/about/ai-premium/).
