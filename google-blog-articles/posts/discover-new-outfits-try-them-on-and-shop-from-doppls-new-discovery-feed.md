We’re introducing a new, shoppable fashion discovery feed in [Doppl](https://labs.google/doppl), an experimental AI app from Google Labs that helps you explore your style and see how new outfits might look on you.

In this new feed, Doppl will provide recommendations so you can discover new favorites and virtually try on items — and nearly everything you see is shoppable, with direct links to merchants.

The discovery feed includes AI-generated videos of real products and recommends outfits based on your personalized style profile, informed by the style preferences you share with Doppl and the items you interact with.

These features are now available on Doppl ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/doppl-google/id6741596720) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.labs.glam)) starting today in the U.S. for people 18+.
