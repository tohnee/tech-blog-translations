In June, Google Labs [introduced](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/dreambeans/) Dreambeans, an experiment that creates personalized daily collections of stories. Now, [Dreambeans](https://labs.google/dreambeans) is available to all accounts
[1](#footnote-1)
in the U.S. (18+) on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.labs.lotus) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dreambeans/id6748778630).

Every dreambean is a combination of:

- **Personalized topics:** This could be everything from places to explore, shows to watch, or a reminder of upcoming events.
- **Connected apps:** Distilled information from the Google apps you select, including Calendar, Gmail, Photos, Search, YouTube, and now Gemini.

If you’ve connected Google Photos, Dreambeans can incorporate visuals of you and your loved ones into your stories and provide thoughtful ways you can engage with them.

Discovered something you like? Dive deeper into any story to learn more, bookmark for later, or easily share with others to spark inspiration.

Share your feedback to make your stories even better over time. Download Dreambeans and start dreaming today.
