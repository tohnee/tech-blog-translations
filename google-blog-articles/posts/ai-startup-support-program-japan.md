The Google AI Futures Fund backs the startups that are building what’s next in AI — and we’re extending our support to Japan. In partnership with Japanese telecom leader KDDI, we’re launching the AI Startup Support Program to accelerate the most innovative AI-native Japanese startups. This initiative is built on our conviction that Japan is home to some of the most visionary AI builders in the world.

Selected startups will receive:

- **Joint funding:** Equity investments from both the AI Futures Fund and KDDI Open Innovation Fund.
- **The latest AI models:** Early access to some of Google’s most advanced AI tools — including Gemini, Nano Banana, and Lyria — plus, opportunities to provide feedback to the researchers building them.
- **Compute credits:** Google Cloud credits, as well as credits for sovereign Gemini and GPUs, uniquely provisioned in Japan via KDDI’s Osaka-Sakai Data Center.
- **Dedicated support:** Hands-on technical support from a network of Google and KDDI researchers, engineers, product managers, and more, as well as distribution and business opportunities.

Learn more about the program on the [AI Futures Fund website](https://labs.google/aifuturesfund/startupsupportprogram). We encourage any Japanese team with an ambitious vision to apply.
