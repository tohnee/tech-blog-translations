An advanced version of Gemini 2.5 Deep Think has achieved gold-medal level performance at the [2025 International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) World Finals](https://worldfinals.icpc.global/). This milestone builds on our [gold-medal win](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/advanced-version-of-gemini-with-deep-think-officially-achieves-gold-medal-standard-at-the-international-mathematical-olympiad/) at the International Mathematical Olympiad in July, underscoring Gemini’s world class capabilities in abstract problem solving. In this year’s ICPC competition, Gemini was able to solve Problem C, a complex optimization task that no university team could solve.

A lightweight version of Gemini 2.5 Deep Think is already available to Google AI Ultra subscribers in the [Gemini app](https://gemini.google/). Learn more about this achievement on the [Google DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/gemini-achieves-gold-level-performance-at-the-international-collegiate-programming-contest).
