[Mixboard](https://labs.google.com/mixboard/welcome) is an experimental, AI-powered concepting board designed to help you explore, expand and refine your ideas. You can bring your own images, or use AI to generate text blocks or create and edit images with Nano Banana, our Gemini image model.

Since launching in September, we’ve seen people use Mixboard to plan parties, design DIY projects, storyboard ideas and more. In response to user feedback, we've made the boards four times the size. Plus, we’re expanding to over 180 additional countries so more people can explore their ideas in Mixboard. See the full list of countries [our support page](https://support.google.com/labs/answer/16689113).
