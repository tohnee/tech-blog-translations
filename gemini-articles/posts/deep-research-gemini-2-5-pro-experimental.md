Gemini Advanced subscribers can now use [Deep Research](https://blog.google/products/gemini/google-gemini-deep-research/) with Gemini 2.5 Pro Experimental, the world’s most capable AI model according to industry [reasoning benchmarks](https://blog.google/technology/google-deepmind/gemini-model-thinking-updates-march-2025/#gemini-2-5-pro) and [Chatbot Arena](https://lmarena.ai/?leaderboard).

Gemini Deep Research is your personal AI research assistant, and with our most intelligent model, it’s even better at every step of the research process. In our testing, raters preferred the reports generated by Gemini Deep Research powered by 2.5 Pro over other leading deep research providers by more than a 2-to-1 margin.

Users testing Deep Research on our latest model are reporting a noticeable improvement in analytical reasoning, information synthesis and generating even more insightful research reports. [Gemini Advanced](https://gemini.google/advanced) users can access it across the web, Android and iOS to generate detailed, easy-to-read reports on just about any research topic, saving hours of time. And don’t forget to try out our Audio Overviews feature where you can turn your report into a podcast-style conversation to hear on the go. [Learn more](https://gemini.google/overview/deep-research) on our website, and try it now by selecting Gemini 2.5 Pro (experimental) from the drop down and tapping “Deep Research” in the prompt bar.

*Note on availability: All Google Workspace users with access to the Gemini app can use Deep Research with Gemini 2.5 Pro Experimental. Deep Research for Google Workspace users is not yet available on the Gemini mobile app.*
