Our new Google DeepMind image generation and editing model (fondly known as [Nano Banana](https://gemini.google/overview/image-generation/?utm_source=keywordblog&utm_medium=referral)) in [the Gemini app](https://gemini.google.com/) opens up a whole wide world of possibility. Want to turn your pet into a tiny figurine, or a video game character? Sure. Eager to have a tea party with your younger self? You’re in luck: This update gives you more control than ever to create the perfect picture. You can keep a person or pet consistent across edits, blend multiple photos together, alter parts of an image while keeping the rest the same and apply the style from one picture’s object to another — all while preserving important details of the original.

For some inspiration, take a look at these examples of what this new model can do.
