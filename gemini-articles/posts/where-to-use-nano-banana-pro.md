We recently launched Nano Banana Pro, our latest image generation and editing model. Nano Banana Pro uses [Gemini 3 Pro](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-3/#gemini-3) to better understand what you want to visualize and generate it with higher precision, more depth and nuance and incredible detail.

## 1. The Gemini app

Nano Banana Pro is available globally in the [Gemini app](https://gemini.google.com/) on desktop or via the mobile app. You can simply select “Create images” and choose the “Thinking” model to use it. There are limits for free-tier users, and the app will go back to using the original Nano Banana after you reach them (Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers receive [higher quotas](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?sjid=8229361629659135193-NC)).

## 2. AI Mode in Search

You can also access Nano Banana Pro through AI Mode in Search. While signed into your account, just head to [AI Mode](http://google.com/ai), select “Thinking with 3 Pro” from the dropdown menu, click the plus sign icon and choose “Create Images Pro.” Describe your image and Nano Banana Pro will take it from there! Image generation in AI Mode is available in select [countries](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16649374#zippy=%2Csupported-countries-and-territories) in English.

## 3. NotebookLM

Nano Banana Pro is available to all [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/) users globally, so you can turn sources and ideas into visually compelling narratives with Slide Decks and Infographics. Slide Decks allow you to instantly convert trusted documents or even just high-level notes into fully styled decks, suitable for both reading and presenting. The feature is especially powerful for synthesizing new information: For example, you can run a [Deep Research](https://blog.google/technology/google-labs/notebooklm-deep-research-file-types/) query, create a Slide Deck and visualize complex topics without wading through dense reports. And if you have an existing draft of a deck, just ask NotebookLM to create more visually appealing slides, even in a certain style of color palette.

## 4. Workspace tools

[Workspace customers](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2025/11/workspace-nano-banana-pro.html) can now use Nano Banana Pro in Google Slides and Google Vids. In Slides, when you open up the Gemini sidebar and select the “Help me visualize” option, it will use the upgraded model to help create infographics, images, charts and more. There’s also a new “Beautify this slide” feature that uses Nano Banana Pro and takes in the context of a slide you created and build on the design. If you like what Nano Banana Pro came up with, select “insert as new slide” to add it to your presentation.

In [Google Vids](https://workspace.google.com/products/vids/), Nano Banana Pro’s upgraded AI generates images with more precision: Now when you choose the “Image” option with the banana icon from the sidebar, if you’re a Workspace customer, you’ll be using Nana Banana Pro — and if you generate a video from scratch, all the visuals will be made using the latest model.

## 5. Flow

Now you can use Nano Banana Pro when you create images in [Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow), our AI filmmaking tool. You can expect higher precision and control over frames and scenes, as well as state-of-the-art text rendering. This is particularly helpful for creatives, who might want to make tweaks like adjusting camera angles, changing focus to create depth or applying sophisticated color grading to set the perfect mood.

Nano Banana Pro is available in Flow for all paid plans.

## 6. Mixboard

[Mixboard](https://labs.google.com/mixboard/welcome) is an experiment from Google Labs to help you explore, expand and refine ideas. With Mixboard’s canvas, you can visualize different ideas using images or text for all kinds of projects, whether that’s a DIY project, a product design or the menu and decor for your holiday party. Nano Banana Pro is available in Mixboard’s newest presentations capability, which transforms your boards into beautifully designed presentations.
