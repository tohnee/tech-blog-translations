[Gemini Drops](https://gemini.google/gemini-drops/) are our regular update on what’s new in the Gemini app. This month, we're focused on new creative and collaboration tools:

- The Nano Banana image editing model has taken the world by storm. See some of the most popular use cases [here](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-nano-banana-examples/).
- Share your camera with [Gemini Live](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-live-updates-august-2025/) and it can highlight exactly what to focus on, giving you the visual guidance you need to get things done in real time. Coming soon to all Android and iOS devices.
- Gemini is coming to users in the U.S. on [Chrome](https://blog.google/products/chrome/new-ai-features-for-chrome/) on desktop. Soon, you'll be able to ask for a quick summary, clarify concepts and find answers using the context of your open tabs.
- You can now share your custom [Gems](https://blog.google/products/gemini/sharing-gems/) with others. Whether you're planning a project or a party, everyone can now use the same AI expertise.
- Create an app in Canvas with no coding experience, and now you can visually edit any part of your web app just by clicking an element and describing the change.
