[Gems](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/google-gems-tips/) help you tailor [Gemini](http://gemini.google.com) to your specific needs, making tasks simpler and saving you time on repetitive prompting. And starting today, you can now share the Gems you make, so your friends, family or coworkers can also try them out.

Whether you’ve crafted a detailed vacation guide for your family, a story-writing partner for your creative team or a personalized meal planner for a friend, sharing your Gems makes collaboration easier.

Sharing Gems works just like sharing files in Google Drive, giving you control over who can view or edit your Gem. To share, simply open your Gem manager on the web and click “Share” next to any Gem you’ve created. Turn your favorite Gems into a shared resource so you can prompt less and create more.
