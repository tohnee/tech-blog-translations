There’s something about the holidays that turns me into a do-er — I always volunteer to make more meals and [host more get-togethers](https://blog.google/products/pixel/google-holiday-hosting-tips-2024/) than I might necessarily have the time to cook or plan. This year is no different, except that I’m using Gemini to cut down on a lot of the time-consuming tasks, so I can enjoy more of the creative, fun parts of holiday prep and planning.

Here are five ways I’m using Gemini to check items off my various holiday to-do lists:

## 1. Brainstorming gift ideas

This year, I plan on getting everyone something they actually want or need — no photo frames or candles here! (Unless they want those, of course.) Gemini is a great brainstorming buddy, so I’m using it to come up with original gift ideas for friends and family. Beyond just asking something like, “What are good gift ideas?,” I can be specific and tell Gemini things that the recipient in question likes to do, how old they are and how well we know each other. And, of course, I can include other parameters like budget and timing.

## 2. Organizing schedules, budgets, gifts and more

The holidays are busy, so it’s extremely possible I might lose track of what’s on my calendar — and maybe even what’s on my shopping list. But I plan on using Gemini’s Google Sheets extension to create templates for things like my schedule, and also for creating a tracking sheet entirely for gifts. I’ll ask Gemini to include tracking for gifts I bought, people I bought them for, how much I spent and more. This way I know not only who’s left to find a gift for but I can also easily see if I’m staying on budget.

## 3. Asking all the cooking questions

I use Gemini as a “kitchen aid” all year, but I spend extra time cooking and baking over the holidays. Instead of frantically texting my mom the night before Thanksgiving for turkey defrosting tips or spending time searching for what cookies I can make with what’s in my pantry and fridge, I can ask Gemini. And I can get super detailed with my requests, too, like by telling it how many people I need to feed (a lot!) and how long I have to prepare (a little!). I also ask for substitution ideas if I can’t make it to the store (butter combined with milk is a good swap for heavy cream, in case you were wondering). Oh, and best yet is that I can do all of this hands-free by using [Gemini Live](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-live-extensions-language-expansion/) (now also [available for iPhone](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-iphone-app/)); that way if (when) I’m covered in flour I can still ask for quick conversions or tips on whether or not to add a pinch of salt.

## 4. Party-planning with a custom Gem

To help with the get-togethers I’m hosting this year, I made a holiday event-planning Gem. [Gems are customized versions of Gemini](https://blog.google/products/gemini/google-gems-tips/) available to Gemini Advanced subscribers that you can give rules to so that they’re fine-tuned for specific uses and so you don’t have to repeatedly set them up with the same information. For example, my Gem knows when my parties are, where I live, about how many people are coming and on and on. ## 5. Writing invites and letters

By the time I sit down to write a note or invite, I probably won’t be able to come up with anything beyond “happy holidays!” Gemini will definitely be helping me with this seasonal writer’s block: Instead of a basic invite, I’ll ask Gemini to come up with a poem for my White Elephant party invites — and I plan on using it to help me write a pun-filled letter to go along with my holiday cards. You can even ask Gemini to help you pen letters to Santa — or from him (don’t worry, Gemini can keep a secret!🧑‍🎄😉).
