[Gemini Drops](https://gemini.google/gemini-drops/) are our regular update on what’s new in the Gemini app. Here's a look at the latest features this month:

- [Gemini 3](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-3-gemini-app/) brings upgraded smarts and new capabilities to the Gemini app: sharper reasoning, a leap in "vibe coding" capabilities, a new look and an experimental agent.
- You can now blend images, design posters and build diagrams with [Nano Banana Pro](https://blog.google/technology/ai/nano-banana-pro/). Then, easily resize your creations for any platform.
- Use [Veo 3.1](https://blog.google/technology/ai/veo-updates-flow/) to generate video with multiple reference images for precise control over characters, objects, style and scene.
- The new [Gemini Agent](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-3-gemini-app/) handles the busywork — from calendars to car rentals — so you can focus on what matters.
- Switch between languages, ask [Gemini Live](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-live-audio-updates/) to talk in a specific speed or tone, or even have it act out things in character.
- We’re also extending our [free year of Google AI Pro to U.S. college students](https://gemini.google/students/), ensuring they have access to the best of Google AI, including Gemini 3.

Find out even more on the [Gemini Drops Hub](https://gemini.google/gemini-drops/).
