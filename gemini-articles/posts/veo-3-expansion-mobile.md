We’re thrilled by the response to [Veo 3](https://deepmind.google/models/veo/). The [Google AI Ultra plan](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/) grants the highest access to Veo 3 and later today we’re launching it in the UK. The Ultra plan is now available in [73 countries](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16126339?sjid=9145468895464903424-NA#video_gen), and we’re working hard to bring it to even more.

Google AI Pro subscribers in those countries get limited access to Veo 3 in [Flow](https://labs.google/flow/about), and a 10-pack of trial video generations to test it out in the [Gemini app](http://gemini.google.com/). Starting today, Pro subscribers will also have access to Veo 3 in the Gemini mobile app.

 It's important that people can access provenance tools for content online. The SynthID watermark is embedded in all content generated by Google's generative AI models. Our [SynthID Detector](https://blog.google/technology/ai/google-synthid-ai-content-detector/) rolled out to early testers last week, and we plan to expand access soon. As an additional step to help people identify AI-generated content, today we're adding a visible watermark to all videos, except for videos generated by Ultra members in Flow, our tool for AI filmmakers.
