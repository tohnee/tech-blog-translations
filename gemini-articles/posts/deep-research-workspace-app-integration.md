One of our most-requested features is here: [Gemini Deep Research](https://gemini.google/overview/deep-research/) can now draw on context from your Gmail, Drive and Chat and work it directly into your research.

This means you can create even more comprehensive reports by pulling in information directly from your Gmail, Drive (including Docs, Slides, Sheets and PDFs) and Google Chat, alongside a variety of sources from the web.

 Now you can start a market analysis for a new product by having Deep Research analyze your team's initial brainstorming docs, related email threads and project plans. Or you can build a competitor report about a rival product that cross-references public web data with your strategies, comparison spreadsheets and team chats.

This powerful new capability is now available for all Gemini users. To get started, just select “Deep Research” from the Tools menu in Gemini on desktop and select your sources. This will begin rolling out to mobile users in the coming days.
