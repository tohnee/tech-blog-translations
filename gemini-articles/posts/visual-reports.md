We’re enhancing Gemini Deep Research to help you visualize complex information instantly. Now available to Google AI Ultra subscribers, Deep Research can go beyond text to generate rich, visual reports complete with custom images, charts and interactive simulations.

Whether you're allocating a marketing budget or exploring complex scientific theories, Gemini can now automatically illustrate your findings. Instead of just reading about a strategy, you can interact with a dynamic simulation model within your report to forecast outcomes based on different variables.

Combining deep analysis with dynamic visuals like diagrams and schematics, Gemini transforms dense data into tangible, easy-to-understand insights.

Select “Deep Research” in the Gemini app prompt bar today to start building more comprehensive reports.
