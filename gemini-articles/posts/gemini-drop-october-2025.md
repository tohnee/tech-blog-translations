[Gemini Drops](https://gemini.google/gemini-drops/) are our regular update on what’s new in the Gemini app. Check out how Gemini app got better this month:

- With [Veo 3.1**,**](https://blog.google/technology/ai/veo-updates-flow/) you can create videos with true-to-life textures, easier camera control and dialogue with sound effects.
- Gemini 2.5 Flash updates help provide step-by-step guidance for complex topics. Plus, get more organized responses and better image understanding for your notes or diagrams.
- In [Canvas](https://gemini.google/overview/canvas/), just give Gemini a topic or upload a source, and it will generate a full set of slides — complete with a theme and relevant images. Then export to Google Slides to easily finalize your presentation.
  [1](#footnote-1)
- We’ve improved LaTeX rendering in Gemini on the web. You can now easily copy formulas, edit directly in Canvas and download as a polished PDFs.
- Gemini can now be your conversational voice assistant to help you find what to watch on TV. Plus, get answers to your biggest questions with supporting YouTube videos with [Gemini for Google TV.](https://blog.google/products/google-tv/gemini-google-tv/)

Find out even more on the [Gemini Drops Hub](https://gemini.google/gemini-drops/).
