With Search interest in “state fair new food” skyrocketing 600% in the last month, it’s clear people are gearing up for one of America’s favorite traditions. Whether you’re hoping to taste iconic foods-on-a-stick, spend your day at the livestock show, catch some live music, or do a little of everything, your state fair day will surely be packed full of things to do.

To help you get the most out of every moment (and every bite), we’re sharing five easy ways Google’s tools can help you make plans, explore, and create memories at the state fair.

## 1. Create your perfect itinerary.

Spend less time planning your route and more time making memories. Just give [Gemini](http://gemini.google.com/) your wishlist — concerts, farm exhibits, shaded breaks — and who you’re bringing along. In seconds, you’ll get a tailored schedule with built-in rest stops designed to maximize the fun and save your feet.

![Gemini crafts the perfect itinerary](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/2026statefair_mobileUIPerfectIti.width-1200.format-webp.webp)

## 2. Take the stress out of getting there and getting around.

Skip the hassle of unexpected driving delays and find what you need on the go. Use real-time information in [Ask Maps](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/ask-maps-immersive-navigation/), our Gemini-powered conversational feature in Google Maps, to check for any incidents or delays on your route before you leave so you don't miss your favorite activities. You can even use Ask Maps to find what you need on-site, like a place to charge your phone or fill up your water bottle.

## 3. Discover the best treats.

The state fair is all about food — think deep-fried classics like corn dogs, and endless sweet treats. (It’s no surprise “new fair foods 2026” is a breakout search!)

Use [AI Mode in Search](http://g.ai/) to discover the top-rated foods-on-a-stick at your local state fair or ask about the best gluten free options. You’ll see a range of top options, helping you skip the guesswork so you can easily find what you’re looking for.

![Gemini helps you find the best treats](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/2026statefair_mobileUIbesttreats.width-1200.format-webp.webp)

## 4. Ask questions about things you see.

Spot something interesting at the fairgrounds? Satisfy your curiosity in real time with [Gemini Live](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-live-camera-how-to/) or [Search Live](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-live-tips/). Just tap the Live icon and point your camera at an exhibit — like Iowa’s famous butter sculpture — to ask about its history. You can even aim your lens at a crowded fair map to quickly find the nearest cool-down zone.

## 5. Turn photos into legendary keepsakes.

Snap a picture of yourself enjoying a funnel cake and ask [Gemini](https://gemini.google/overview/image-generation/) to render it as a vintage 1950s postcard. Or take a photo of the rodeo arena and ask Gemini to place you in the center of the action as a championship bull rider. All it takes is a photo and a little imagination to create memorable and sharable content that captures the fun of the day.

Google is excited to visit state fairs this summer! Swing by the Gemini Visitor Centers at the state fairs in Iowa (August 13-15), Minnesota (August 27-29), or Texas (October 9-10) to try this out for yourself.
