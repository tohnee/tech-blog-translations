Conversations are about more than just words. They’re about the nuance in how we speak — the rise and fall of our voices, the rhythm of our sentences and the tone behind our words. When you have a conversation with Gemini
[1](#footnote-1)
, we want it to feel just as natural and intuitive.

At Made by Google, we [announced](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-live-updates-august-2025/) new model updates coming to Gemini Live that dramatically improve its ability to understand and use key elements of human speech. Starting today, you can experience more adaptive and expressive conversations, giving you control and opening up new ways to learn and practice skills on both Android and iOS.

Here are five ways you can take advantage of these new updates:

## 1. Tailor your learning.

Imagine you're a student tackling a complex subject like business analytics. You can ask Gemini to explain a topic in your lesson plan and then say, "Okay, speed up," to get a crash course on the way to your next class. This feature also increases accessibility, allowing users to adjust Gemini's speech to a more comfortable and comprehensible speed. It's all about putting you in complete control of your learning environment.

## 2. Immerse yourself in a new language.

Learning a new language goes beyond just memorizing vocabulary. With this update, you can now get tailored practice in the areas you need it most. Ask Gemini to quiz you on multiples of 10 in Korean, or practice casual greetings in Spanish. This allows you to gain real-world speaking experience in a low-risk setting, helping you build confidence before you use your skills in real life.

## 3. Practice for your next big moment.

Gemini can help you practice for your next big moment, whether you’re rehearsing for a job interview or preparing for a difficult conversation. Gemini's ability to respond to your situation creates an approachable practice environment to build your confidence and refine your communication skills.

## 4. Hear stories come to life.

Get ready for story time to be more engaging than ever. Ask Gemini to tell you about the Roman empire from the perspective of Julius Caesar himself, and get a rich, engaging narrative complete with character accents. This new level of dramatic storytelling makes learning and entertainment more immersive.

## 5. Liven things up with a fun accent.

Sometimes you just want to have a little fun. You can ask Gemini to speak in a fun accent to liven things up. Try out a cowboy accent when brainstorming dessert ideas for a rodeo-themed birthday party or have Gemini talk you through a Sunday roast recipe in a Cockney British accent. This adds a new layer of personality, making it more enjoyable to get things done while talking to Gemini.

These updates are designed to make conversations with Gemini more intuitive and effective. Try them out today in [Gemini Live](https://gemini.google/overview/gemini-live/).
