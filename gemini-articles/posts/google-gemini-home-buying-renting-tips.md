Moving season peaks between May and August in the U.S. In fact, Google Trends show a 350% spike in 'homes for sale near me' searches over the past month. So if you’re a homebuyer or renter looking for your new home, the next few months could be a whirlwind of spreadsheets, inspection reports and open houses. The process can be as overwhelming as it is exciting.

But as the market heats up, a growing number of people are offloading that heavy lifting to AI. Whether you're vetting a new neighborhood, visualizing a renovation or just trying to find a pet-friendly apartment, here are six ways [Gemini](http://gemini.google.com/) can help.

## 1. Visualize renovations

Found a place with "good bones" but a questionable design? Upload photos or floor plans to virtually swap out flooring, try on new paint colors and test different layouts. Gemini can help you identify design styles you like and even point out structural possibilities before you commit to a purchase or lease.

## 2. Summarize long, complex documents

Turn that 40-page inspection report or lengthy lease into a concise list of the most expensive structural concerns for your negotiations or a TL;DR of what to know before you sign. You can also ask Gemini to flag specific restrictions on pets, solar panels or EV charging that might be buried deep in dense HOA bylaws.

## 3. Streamline your online search

While browsing listing sites, use Gemini in Chrome to act as your personal house hunting assistant. Ask Gemini to filter for specific needs that the site's filters might not cover, like "apartments with floor-to-ceiling windows."

Tell Gemini in Chrome what you need and watch it handle the task, while keeping you in control.

## 4. Find details buried in your inbox

Where was that contractor quote? What were the terms of that rental agreement? Use AI Overviews in Gmail to surface specific documents and details from months of correspondence. Just ask Gemini in natural language to find what you need without having to manually search through old email threads.

Get an [AI Overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZK2DjlvBDuo) with the key details when you ask your inbox to find renovation quotes from a year ago.

## 5. Research a neighborhood

Start by pasting listing descriptions to decode buzzwords that might hide underlying issues (what does "cozy" really mean?). Gemini can also help you analyze hyper-local market trends to get a feel for a neighborhood's vibe, from typical noise levels to the proximity of the nearest dog park.

## 6. Model your finances

Buying a home comes with a lot of number-crunching. You can use Gemini to run "what-if" mortgage scenarios or calculate the projected ROI on a specific remodel based on local comps. Gemini can even generate custom Google Sheets templates to help you track closing costs and appraisal fees.

Moving is a big decision, but the process doesn’t have to be overwhelming thanks to a little help from Gemini.
