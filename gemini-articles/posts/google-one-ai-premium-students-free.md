## Gemini Advanced for exam prep, homework and writing help

[Gemini Advanced](https://gemini.google/advanced/?hl=en) is great for helping inside and outside the classroom. It’s powered by Gemini 2.5 Pro, [our most intelligent AI model](https://lmarena.ai/?leaderboard), and it’s available on your phone or desktop. It’s full of features that are helpful for students like [Canvas](https://gemini.google/overview/canvas/?hl=en), a writing companion, and [Gemini Live](https://gemini.google/overview/gemini-live/?hl=en), a free-flowing conversational assistant that helps you talk out your ideas.

## Gemini Deep Research to go in depth

Gemini’s [Deep Research](https://gemini.google/overview/deep-research/?hl=en) is great at exploring complex topics and coming back with easy-to-read reports. In our testing, [raters preferred](https://blog.google/products/gemini/deep-research-gemini-2-5-pro-experimental/) Gemini Deep Research on 2.5 Pro Experimental by a 2-to-1 margin over the next best competitor. You can also convert your report into a podcast-style audio overview and listen to a deep dive on any topic while you’re walking between classes.

## NotebookLM Plus as a tool for understanding

[NotebookLM Plus](https://notebooklm.google/) is an innovative thinking companion from Google. When you upload your class syllabus or research sources, it becomes an instant expert on that information. With one tap, you can create an Audio Overview, make a visual mind map or generate a study guide in seconds. Students can also discover new study materials directly within NotebookLM using the new [Discover Sources](https://blog.google/technology/google-labs/notebooklm-discover-sources/) feature.

## Gemini as your AI Assistant in Google Docs, Sheets and Slides

Get help with your projects using Gemini built into Google Docs, Slides and Sheets. In Docs, Gemini acts as a writing partner to help you perfect your papers. In Sheets, it helps you analyze and visualize data faster. And in Slides, you can quickly create presentations or generate images for your slides.

## Veo 2 in Gemini and Whisk to unleash your creativity

Veo 2 in Gemini Advanced lets you create incredibly detailed eight-second video clips from anything you can describe with words. And [Whisk](https://labs.google/fx/tools/whisk), a new tool from Google Labs, lets you mix text and image prompts to create something new, and now you can animate your creations as well.
