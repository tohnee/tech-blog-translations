[Gemini 3.8 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/) is our most intelligent workhorse model, delivering significant improvements from 3.7 Flash across software engineering, agentic tasks, and critical, multistep reasoning in specialized domains. These performance gains stem from a core design choice: 3.8 Flash works harder. On complex tasks, it exhibits greater diligence — executing extra reasoning steps and calling tools iteratively to land a more accurate output.

Builders have been creating with [Gemini 3.8 Flash](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/flash/) since we launched it and [new audio models](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-text-to-speech/) this month. From developing simulations to testing visual reasoning, check out some of the coolest builds and experiments from the community so far.

## Create a model rocket simulation.

[Ashutosh Shrivastava (@ai\_for\_success on X)](https://x.com/ai_for_success) used Gemini 3.8 Flash and [Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/) to map the live paths of satellites, space stations, and orbital rockets.

## Animate an ink painting.

[Noctus (@noctus91 on X)](https://x.com/noctus91) used Gemini 3.8 Flash’s multimodal capabilities to transform Seigaiha waves — a traditional Japanese pattern — into what looks like a moving ink painting.

## Create a 3D dinosaur skeleton.

Gemini 3.8 Flash delivers substantial gains on multistep reasoning in STEM and other fields. [Emily (@IamEmily2050 on X)](https://x.com/IamEmily2050) pushed these capabilities, asking the model to create a T. rex skeleton with a four-phase prompt, complete with strict requirements, banned shortcuts, and accuracy checks.

## Build an automatic transmission simulation from scratch.

[Hakm (@hakmgpt on X)](https://x.com/hakmgpt) gave Gemini 3.8 Flash an engineering challenge: Build an interactive model of an automatic transmission. His prototype involves 10 camera views, four display modes, smart labels, and a side panel that teaches you more about any transmission part you click on.

Gemini 3.8 Flash delivers substantial gains from 3.7 Flash, often approaching the performance of higher-cost frontier models. Try it across Google products and tools, including [Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/) and [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/prompts/new_chat?model=gemini-3.8-flash).
