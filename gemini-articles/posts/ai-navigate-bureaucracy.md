It turns out navigating bureaucracy is one of the biggest reasons people turn to AI. Think administrative chores, like renewing a driver’s license, deciphering tax requirements, or applying for local services. According to [Google’s AI & Economy ATLAS v1.0 study](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/understanding-the-ai-economy/), these tedious tasks take up a fraction of our day, yet drive a disproportionate amount of our AI usage. Nearly half of these conversations happen outside of standard business hours, helping people get answers even when government offices are closed.

Here are four ways you can use AI to sift through official documents and navigate complex government systems.
[1](#footnote-1)

## 1. Filing forms and paying fees

When it’s time to renew your license, file your taxes, or pay fines and fees, understanding what you need to do is often surprisingly hard. What does the jargon on that form actually mean? And what are the exact steps you need to follow to cross that task off your to-do list? About 40% of people using Gemini for civic tasks consult AI for queries like these.

Gemini acts as a translator for government language, helping you break down step-by-step requirements before you fill out forms or head to a local agency.

- **Try this prompt:** [I’m registering a newly purchased used car in New York. Can you give me a simple, step-by-step checklist of the documents, fees, and forms I need to prepare before visiting the DMV?](https://gemini.google.com/app/download/mobile?android-min-version=301356232&ios-min-version=322.0&is_sa=1&pt=9008&mt=8&prompt_action=autosubmit&prompt_id=4wU2haRdguaP&utm_medium=blog&utm_source=gemini&utm_campaign=civic_blog_q3&campaign_id=civic_blog_q3&ct=civic_blog)

## 2. Participating in civic life

From preparing for jury duty to checking community guidelines to understanding voting procedures, staying informed and actively participating in your community takes time. And about 26% of people using Gemini for government tasks rely on AI to get up to speed.

Gemini can help you by breaking down local rules and outlining compliance procedures.

- **Try this prompt:** [I have upcoming jury duty in Chicago for the first time. What should I expect from the selection process, what to bring, and general court protocols?](https://gemini.google.com/app/download/mobile?android-min-version=301356232&ios-min-version=322.0&is_sa=1&pt=9008&mt=8&prompt_action=autosubmit&prompt_id=wREm04t9QOfb&utm_medium=blog&utm_source=gemini&utm_campaign=civic_blog_q3&campaign_id=civic_blog_q3&ct=civic_blog)

## 3. Finding government agencies and regulations

Tracking down the right government department can be daunting. And when you need to look up public records or local zoning rules, you might end up jumping around agency websites trying to find what you need. Over 16% of people using Gemini for civic chores turn to AI to streamline this process.

Since Gemini synthesizes municipal information, it can answer your questions and help you figure out where to go to learn more.

- **Try this prompt:** [What are the general steps and typical documentation needed to file a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request for local municipal records?](https://gemini.google.com/app/download/mobile?android-min-version=301356232&ios-min-version=322.0&is_sa=1&pt=9008&mt=8&prompt_action=autosubmit&prompt_id=CW6GujdLYRvQ&utm_medium=blog&utm_source=gemini&utm_campaign=civic_blog_q3&campaign_id=civic_blog_q3&ct=civic_blog)

## 4. Accessing social services

Even once you’ve found the right agency, it’s not always easy to determine whether you’re eligible for benefits, like disability, Social Security, or public assistance. More than 15% of people using Gemini for government tasks ask AI for help navigating these systems.

Gemini can summarize the eligibility criteria for different social and public services so you can prepare your applications with confidence.

- **Try this prompt:** [Can you explain how the Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) application process works and list the key medical and work history documents needed to apply?](https://gemini.google.com/app/download/mobile?android-min-version=301356232&ios-min-version=322.0&is_sa=1&pt=9008&mt=8&prompt_action=autosubmit&prompt_id=qK9MdB5rfBOg&utm_medium=blog&utm_source=gemini&utm_campaign=civic_blog_q3&campaign_id=civic_blog_q3&ct=civic_blog)

Over time, time spent on juggling paperwork and parsing regulations can really add up. By reducing that so-called “bureaucracy tax,” Gemini helps you get things done, stay informed, and access crucial services — in a way that works for you and your schedule.
