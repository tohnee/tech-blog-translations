# Bringing real-time market sentiment to Tori, from eToro

Tori, eToro’s AI agent, uses models from SpaceXAI to embed real-time market sentiment directly into Tori’s investing workflow.

---

[Open API docs](https://docs.x.ai/)

Tori, eToro’s AI agent, uses models from SpaceXAI to embed real-time market sentiment directly into Tori’s investing workflow.

SpaceXAI and [eToro](https://www.etoro.com/) are proud to announce that Tori, an AI agent, is now powered by real-time information from X. Tori can now read market moods as they shift, track live signals and analyze information with SpaceXAI's state-of-the-art text models.

## [Markets Move at the Speed of X](#markets-move-at-the-speed-of-x)

The latest information is out first on X. SpaceXAI's models and platform bring first party support to that platform, thereby enabling eToro users to ask and analyze market sentiment using Tori.

For close to two decades, eToro has built a place where people invest together, and today has more than 40 million registered users across 75 countries. Powering Tori with SpaceXAI models furthers this vision to bring the best information on demand to all users.

## [Built on SpaceXAI APIs](#built-on-spacexai-apis)

The same real-time intelligence and live X awareness helping to power Tori is available to any team through the [API console](https://console.x.ai/). Whether you're building a research copilot, a sentiment dashboard, or an assistant for your daily briefings, you can get set up in minutes through the API console.
