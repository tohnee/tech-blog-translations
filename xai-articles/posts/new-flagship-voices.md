# 21 New Flagship Grok Voices

New multilingual voices for Grok Voice, plus improved naturalness for the original five.

[Try It Free](https://console.x.ai/team/default/voice/agents?campaign=new-voices-blog)[Try in Playground](https://console.x.ai/team/default/voice/text-to-speech?campaign=new-voices-blog)

Today, we're releasing **21 new flagship voices** for Grok Voice, joining the original five. All of them are multilingual and available now in the realtime [Voice Agent API](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/audio/voice-agent?campaign=new-voices-blog), the [Text to Speech API](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/audio/text-to-speech?campaign=new-voices-blog), and the new [**Grok Voice Agent Builder**](https://console.x.ai/team/default/voice/agents?campaign=new-voices-blog).
