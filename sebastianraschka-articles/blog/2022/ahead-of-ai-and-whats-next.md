# Ahead Of AI, And What’s Next?

## The joy of writing

As you may know, I love reading and writing! I’ve been a more or less avid blogger for more than a decade and have also published several books since then. I can’t help it: I love writing about my favorite
subjects – machine learning and coding.

![Ahead of ai and whats next red robin writing on the moon](https://sebastianraschka.com/images/blog/2022/ahead-of-ai-and-whats-next/red-robin-writing-on-the-moon.webp)

I am planning to continue this blog indefinitely. Nonetheless, there are many smaller but exciting topics I’d love to share and write about that don’t fit the blog format. So, trying to find a compromise, I thought that a monthly newsletter would be the perfect solution!

Thus, I am excited to share the launch of Ahead Of AI, a monthly letter recapping interesting trends and articles related to AI. However, in addition to just musing about the latest developments in AI, the newsletter features many other sections, including educational nuggets, machine learning quiz questions, my favorite productivity tips, humor, and more! I hope you’ll like it. Sounds interesting? You can [read and subscribe to it here](https://newsletter.sebastianraschka.com).

[![Ahead of ai and whats next Ahead of AI Main Logo](https://sebastianraschka.com/images/blog/2022/ahead-of-ai-and-whats-next/Ahead-of-AI-Main-Logo.webp)](https://newsletter.sebastianraschka.com)

## What I am currently up to

I am working on a huge passion project of mine that I think you will find very fun and exciting as well! The ETA is Nov/Dec 2022 (if things go smoothly), so you will hear more about that very, very soon!

In the meantime, I am also working on bridging the gap between my academic life as a deep learning researcher and learning about deep learning for real-world applications.

Coming from academia, discovering and learning about how production system work is like discovering a whole new world! To give you a brief glimpse, check out our recent article on [Diffusion Models in Production](https://lightning.ai/pages/community/tutorial/deploy-diffusion-models/).

PS: I will be at both [NeurIPS 2022](https://neurips.cc) (co-hosting a social) and the [Python Conference 2022](https://pytorch.org/blog/announcing-pytorch-conference-2022/) in December! I am looking forward to meet one or the other reader there!
